LESSON 15 — A1 CONSOLIDATION: A DAY IN THE CITY

Lesson goals:
  • Read a longer A1 text that uses everything from the level.
  • See ser vs estar, the present tense and reflexive verbs working together.
  • Review the city, transport, weather and hobbies in one story.
  • Pick up a few high-frequency words: siempre, nunca, cada, tranquilo.

Core vocabulary: pasear, caminar, padres, hermoso, cada, siempre, nunca, pasar, tranquilo, rutina
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): diario, costumbre, terminar, ocupado, andar

1. READING PASSAGE (A DAY IN THE LIFE OF MARTA)

Context: Marta describes a normal day, from morning to night. This text reuses nearly all of A1. Read each line; the English below it is only a check.

(Read top to bottom — let the Spanish carry you.)

Hola. Me llamo Marta y vivo en una ciudad del sur de España.

Hi. My name is Marta and I live in a city in the south of Spain.

Mi rutina es tranquila. Cada mañana me levanto a las siete y media.

My routine is calm. Every morning I get up at half past seven.

Me ducho, desayuno en la cocina y salgo de casa a las ocho.

I shower, have breakfast in the kitchen and leave the house at eight.

Voy al trabajo en autobús. Trabajo en una oficina en el centro, al lado de un parque.

I go to work by bus. I work in an office in the centre, next to a park.

A las dos como con mi familia: sopa, pescado y fruta.

At two I have lunch with my family: soup, fish and fruit.

Por la tarde, si hace sol, paseo por la ciudad con mi hermana.

In the afternoon, if it's sunny, I stroll around the city with my sister.

Nos gusta caminar hasta la plaza, comprar fruta en el mercado y tomar un café.

We like to walk to the square, buy fruit at the market and have a coffee.

En verano hace mucho calor y la gente sale por la noche.

In summer it's very hot and people go out at night.

Hoy está nublado y llueve un poco, así que prefiero quedarme en casa y leer.

Today it's cloudy and it's raining a little, so I prefer to stay at home and read.

Por la noche ceno con mis padres, veo una película y me acuesto a las once.

At night I have dinner with my parents, watch a film and go to bed at eleven.

Mañana es sábado: no trabajo, así que quiero ir a la playa con mis amigos.

Tomorrow is Saturday: I don't work, so I want to go to the beach with my friends.

El español es un idioma hermoso y cada día entiendo más.

Spanish is a beautiful language and every day I understand more.

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR (A1 AT A GLANCE)

This is a revision map of Level A1. Read it as a checklist; each point comes from an earlier lesson.

2.1. ser vs estar vs hay

FormUseExample
seridentity, origin, permanent traitsSoy de Vietnam. Es un idioma hermoso.
estarlocation and temporary statesEl museo está cerca. Está nublado.
haythere is / there areHay un parque en mi barrio.

2.2. The present tense

Regular -ar / -er / -ir verbs, plus the stem-changing verbs and reflexes you have met:

  • hablo / como / vivoI speak / I eat / I live (regular)
  • quiero, puedo, pido, prefiero, vuelvostem-changing verbs (Lesson 10)
  • me levanto, me ducho, me acuestoreflexive verbs of the daily routine (Lesson 08)

2.3. ir a + place and the near future

  • Voy al centro en autobús.I go to the centre by bus.
  • Quiero ir a la playa con mis amigos.I want to go to the beach with my friends.

2.4. Likes with gustar

  • Nos gusta caminar y tomar un café.We like walking and having a coffee.
  • Me gusta la música; me gustan las películas.I like music; I like films.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences that summarize A1. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Introduce yourself and your home:

Me llamo Marta, soy de España y vivo en el sur.

My name is Marta, I'm from Spain and I live in the south.

2. Describe your routine:

Cada día me levanto, trabajo y vuelvo a casa.

Every day I get up, work and come back home.

3. Talk about the city:

Mi barrio es tranquilo y hay un parque cerca.

My neighbourhood is quiet and there's a park nearby.

4. Talk about the weather:

Hoy hace sol, pero mañana llueve.

Today it's sunny, but tomorrow it rains.

5. Say what you like:

Me gusta pasear y me encanta leer.

I like strolling and I love reading.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two friends plan a Saturday together.

Diego: ¿Qué haces el sábado?

Diego: What are you doing on Saturday?

Marta: No trabajo. Por la mañana paseo por el centro.

Marta: I'm not working. In the morning I stroll around the centre.

Diego: ¿Qué tiempo hace?

Diego: What's the weather like?

Marta: Hace sol y no hace mucho calor. Perfecto para caminar.

Marta: It's sunny and not very hot. Perfect for walking.

Diego: ¿Y por la tarde?

Diego: And in the afternoon?

Marta: Me gusta ir al cine. ¿Vienes conmigo?

Marta: I like going to the cinema. Are you coming with me?

Diego: ¡Claro! A mí también me gustan las películas.

Diego: Of course! I like films too.

New words in the dialogue: perfecto (perfect), conmigo (with me), vienes (you come, from venir).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • ser = identity/origin; estar = location/state; hay = there is/are.
  • The present tense carries most of A1: regular verbs, stem-changing verbs and reflexes.
  • ir a + place; gustar / encantar for likes.
  • Everyday topics: city, transport, weather, hobbies, routine.
  • New frequency words: siempre, nunca, cada, pasar.

5.2. Further reading — a letter from Hanoi

Context: A short letter that mirrors Marta's day, from the learner's own city. Read it quietly:

Hola, Marta. Soy Nam y vivo en Hanoi, en el norte de Vietnam.

Hello, Marta. I'm Nam and I live in Hanoi, in the north of Vietnam.

Mi rutina también es tranquila. Cada día trabajo en una oficina cerca del centro.

My routine is calm too. Every day I work in an office near the centre.

En Hanoi hace calor y llueve mucho en verano.

In Hanoi it's hot and it rains a lot in summer.

Me gusta caminar por el parque con mis amigos y beber café.

I like walking in the park with my friends and drinking coffee.

Ahora estudio español y cada día entiendo más. ¡Hasta pronto!

Now I'm studying Spanish and every day I understand more. See you soon!

New words: Nam (a Vietnamese name), Hanoi (the capital of Vietnam), ¡Hasta pronto! (See you soon!).
