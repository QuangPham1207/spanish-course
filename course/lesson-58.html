LESSON 58 — UNREAL & MIXED CONDITIONALS

Lesson goals:
  • Build present-unreal conditionals: si tuviera… haría…
  • Build past-unreal conditionals: si hubiera… habría…
  • Mix the halves: past condition with present result, and back.
  • Read an alternate-history vignette: what if the train had arrived?
  • Pick up words: desvío, desenlace, bifurcación, comarca, pregonero, apeadero, maquinista.

Core vocabulary: tuviera, hubiera, supiera, hiciera, diera, estuviera, desvío, desenlace, bifurcación, comarca, cacique, alcalde, pregonero, campanario, locomotora, vagón, apeadero, hundir, herrero, taberna, vía, maquinista, cambiar, curso, azar, fatalidad, designio, arrepentimiento, amargura, desengaño, promesa, juramento, trato, apuesta, imaginarse, farol
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): badajo, cencerro, romería, quinqué

1. READING PASSAGE (EL TREN QUE NUNCA LLEGÓ)

Context: San Miguel del Monte is a quiet village today. But in 1911 the railway almost arrived — and the village still wonders what would have happened if it had. Read each line; the English below it is only a check.

(Unreal pasts fill this story — if it had arrived, everything would have changed… is today's grammar.)

San Miguel del Monte es hoy un pueblo tranquilo de la comarca, famoso por su queso de oveja y por su romería de octubre, cuando todo el pueblo sale al campo.

San Miguel del Monte is today a quiet village of the district, famous for its sheep cheese and its October pilgrimage festival, when the whole village goes out to the countryside.

Pero en 1911 todo pudo ser distinto: la compañía quería llevar la vía hasta el pueblo y cambiar el curso de la comarca, y soñaba con fábricas, con escuelas y con calles llenas de gente.

But in 1911 everything could have been different: the company wanted to bring the track to the village and change the course of the district, and dreamed of factories, schools and streets full of people.

El alcalde y el cacique cerraron un trato en la taberna, a la luz de un quinqué, entre vasos de vino y papeles firmados a medias.

The mayor and the local boss sealed a deal in the tavern, by the light of an oil lamp, among glasses of wine and half-signed papers.

Hicieron una apuesta: si el tren llegaba antes de Navidad, el cacique pagaba la fiesta; si no, pagaba el alcalde.

They made a bet: if the train arrived before Christmas, the boss would pay for the fiesta; if not, the mayor would pay.

El joven maquinista prometió traer la locomotora nueva tirando de un solo vagón de regalos, con un farol encendido y un cencerro atado atrás, por broma, y los niños aplaudían al ver pasar la máquina negra.

The young engine driver promised to bring the new locomotive pulling a single wagon of gifts, with a lamp lit and a cowbell tied at the back, as a joke — and the children clapped as they watched the black machine go by.

El pregonero anunció la promesa desde el campanario, aunque el badajo estaba roto y la campana sonaba ronca y triste.

The town crier announced the promise from the bell tower, even though the clapper was broken and the bell sounded hoarse and sad.

Si el pueblo tuviera su apeadero, decían, los jóvenes no se irían a la ciudad.

If the village had its halt, they said, young people wouldn't leave for the city.

Si mi abuelo supiera leer los planos, habría visto el error del ingeniero.

If my grandfather had known how to read blueprints, he would have seen the engineer's mistake.

El ingeniero marcó mal un desvío en la bifurcación del valle, y nadie revisó su trabajo.

The engineer mis-marked a switch at the fork of the valley, and nobody checked his work.

La riada de noviembre logró hundir el puente nuevo antes del primer viaje, con todo el pueblo mirando desde la plaza.

The November flood managed to sink the new bridge before the first journey, with the whole village watching from the square.

Si la riada no hubiera llegado aquella noche, el puente seguiría en pie.

If the flood hadn't come that night, the bridge would still be standing.

Si el alcalde hubiera firmado el contrato a tiempo, la compañía habría pagado otro puente.

If the mayor had signed the contract on time, the company would have paid for another bridge.

Pero el alcalde no firmó: dijo que si diera su firma, el cacique se quedaría con todo, hasta con la taberna y las tierras del valle.

But the mayor didn't sign: he said that if he gave his signature, the boss would keep everything, even the tavern and the valley lands.

Si el ingeniero hiciera hoy el mismo plano, cualquier alumno vería el error.

If the engineer drew the same blueprint today, any schoolchild would see the mistake.

El herrero, que había hecho un juramento solemne, lloró de amargura ante su familia y no trabajó en tres días.

The blacksmith, who had made a solemn vow, wept with bitterness before his family and didn't work for three days.

Si el pueblo estuviera hoy en la línea principal, tendría hospital y universidad.

If the village were on the main line today, it would have a hospital and a university.

Los viejos dicen que fue el azar, otros hablan de fatalidad; mi abuela habla del designio de Dios, que nadie puede cambiar.

Old people say it was chance, others speak of fate; my grandmother speaks of God's design, which nobody can change.

Solo el tren fantasma visita el pueblo: para imaginarse su silbido hay que cerrar los ojos en la vía muerta y escuchar el viento entre los árboles.

Only the ghost train visits the village: to imagine its whistle you have to close your eyes on the disused track and listen to the wind in the trees.

Qué desengaño para todos, y qué arrepentimiento para el alcalde: aquel fue el desenlace de la historia del tren en San Miguel.

What a disappointment for everyone, and what regret for the mayor: that was the ending of the train story in San Miguel.

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. Present-unreal: si + imperfect subjunctive + conditional

Dreams about a different present or future: the si-clause takes the imperfect subjunctive (L57), and the result takes the conditional. Order of halves doesn't matter.

si + imperfect subjunctive + conditional → si tuviera… no se irían
  • Si el pueblo tuviera su apeadero, los jóvenes no se irían.If the village had its halt, young people wouldn't leave.
  • Si el pueblo estuviera en la línea principal, tendría hospital.If the village were on the main line, it would have a hospital.
  • Si el ingeniero hiciera hoy el plano, cualquier alumno vería el error.If the engineer drew the blueprint today, any schoolchild would see the mistake.
  • Dijo que si diera su firma, el cacique se quedaría con todo.He said that if he gave his signature, the boss would keep everything.

2.2. Past-unreal: si + pluperfect subjunctive + conditional perfect

Dreams about a different past: the si-clause takes hubiera + participle (full forms in L59), and the result takes habría + participle. Both halves look backward.

si + hubiera + participle + habría + participle → si hubiera firmado… habría pagado
  • Si el alcalde hubiera firmado, la compañía habría pagado otro puente.If the mayor had signed, the company would have paid for another bridge.
  • Si mi abuelo supiera leer los planos, habría visto el error.If my grandfather had known how to read blueprints, he would have seen the mistake. (mixed: past result, see 2.3)
  • Si la riada no hubiera llegado, el puente seguiría en pie.If the flood hadn't come, the bridge would still be standing. (mixed: present result)
💡 Spain-vs-LatAm note: this whole system is identical everywhere — the train story works the same in Madrid and in México. Only the endings vary: hubiera dominates in speech on both sides; hubiese sounds more literary, a little more so in Latin America.

2.3. Mixed conditionals: borrow each half where you need it

Real thinking mixes clocks: a past condition with a present result, or a present condition with a past result. Just combine the halves from 2.1 and 2.2.

past condition + present result · or · present condition + past result
  • Si la riada no hubiera llegado, el puente seguiría en pie.If the flood hadn't come, the bridge would still be standing. (then → now)
  • Si mi abuelo supiera leer los planos, habría visto el error.If my grandfather knew how to read blueprints, he would have seen the mistake. (now → then)
  • Si el pueblo estuviera en la línea, hoy habríamos nacido en otro pueblo.If the village were on the line, today we would have been born in a different village. (now → then)
⚠️ Common mistakes: the si-clause never takes a conditional: *si tendría, *si habría llegado are wrong — always si tuviera, si hubiera llegado. And the result never takes a subjunctive: *si tuviera, pagara is wrong — the result needs pagaría / habría pagado.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five unreal sentences. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Present dream:

Si tuviera tiempo, vendría.

If I had time, I'd come.

2. Past dream:

Si hubiera llegado el tren, todo habría cambiado.

If the train had arrived, everything would have changed.

3. Knowing:

Si supiera la verdad, te la diría.

If I knew the truth, I'd tell you.

4. Giving:

Si me diera su firma, todo se arreglaría.

If he gave me his signature, everything would be fixed.

5. Weather:

Si hiciera buen tiempo, iríamos a la romería.

If the weather were good, we'd go to the pilgrimage festival.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two neighbours, Julián and Rosa, drink wine in the taberna and replay the old story.

Julián: Si el tren hubiera llegado, yo habría nacido en otro pueblo.

Julián: If the train had arrived, I would have been born in a different village.

Rosa: Y si tu abuelo supiera leer planos, sería ingeniero.

Rosa: And if your grandfather knew how to read blueprints, he'd be an engineer.

Julián: Hicimos una apuesta en esta taberna, ¿te acuerdas?

Julián: We made a bet in this tavern, remember?

Rosa: Sí: si ganaba el alcalde, pagaba la fiesta.

Rosa: Yes: if the mayor won, he'd pay for the fiesta.

Julián: Qué desengaño, y qué desenlace tan triste.

Julián: What a disappointment, and what a sad ending.

Rosa: No fue el azar, fue la fatalidad del río.

Rosa: It wasn't chance, it was the fatefulness of the river.

Julián: Pues brindemos por el trato: que vuelva el tren, aunque sea fantasma.

Julián: Then let's drink to the deal: may the train return, even as a ghost.

No new words — the dialogue reuses apuesta (bet), taberna (tavern), desenlace (ending), azar (chance) and trato (deal) from today's table.

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • Present-unreal: si + imperfect subjunctive + conditional (si tuviera… no se irían).
  • Past-unreal: si + hubiera + participle + habría + participle (si hubiera firmado… habría pagado).
  • Mixed: combine halves freely — past condition with present result (seguiría en pie), present condition with past result (habría visto).

5.2. Further reading — el tren fantasma

Context: What the village hears at night. Read it quietly:

Dicen que de noche se oye un silbido junto a la vía muerta.

They say that at night a whistle is heard by the disused track.

Si te quedas allí a las doce, verás un farol entre la niebla.

If you stay there at midnight, you'll see a lamp in the mist.

Sería el maquinista fantasma, que sigue buscando su apeadero.

It would be the ghost engine driver, still looking for his halt.

Para imaginarse la escena hay que cerrar los ojos y escuchar el campanario.

To imagine the scene you have to close your eyes and listen to the bell tower.

Y si el tren volviera, el pueblo haría fiesta como en la romería.

And if the train came back, the village would party as at the pilgrimage festival.

No new words — it reuses vía (track), farol (lamp), maquinista (engine driver), apeadero (halt), imaginarse (to imagine), campanario (bell tower) and romería from today's table.
