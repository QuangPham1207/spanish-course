LESSON 22 — HEALTH & THE BODY

Lesson goals:
  • Name the parts of the body: cabeza, brazo, pierna, estómago….
  • Describe symptoms with doler: me duele la cabeza / me duelen los pies.
  • Talk about being ill: tener fiebre, tos, un resfriado; estar enfermo.
  • Give advice with deber + infinitivo and tener que + infinitivo.

Core vocabulary: cuerpo, cabeza, ojo, nariz, boca, oreja, cuello, brazo, dedo, pierna, pie, espalda, estómago, garganta, pecho, corazón, dolor, doler, fiebre, tos, resfriado, enfermo/enferma, sano/sana, salud, cuidarse, deber
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): toser, gripe, pastilla, medicina, consulta, síntoma, receta, oído

1. READING PASSAGE (ANA ESTÁ ENFERMA)

Context: Ana is ill and visits the doctor. Words for the body and for illness are in bold. Read each line; the English below is only a check.

(Watch how Spanish says "my head hurts": it uses an article, not a possessive.)

Ayer Ana no fue a clase porque se sentía mal.

Yesterday Ana didn't go to class because she felt bad.

Le dolía la cabeza y tenía fiebre.

She had a headache and a fever.

También le dolía la garganta y tosía mucho.

Her throat hurt too and she was coughing a lot.

Su madre le dijo: «Debes ir al médico.»

Her mother told her: "You should go to the doctor."

En la consulta, la doctora le preguntó qué le pasaba.

At the surgery, the doctor asked her what was wrong.

Ana explicó sus síntomas: dolor de cabeza, tos y mucho cansancio.

Ana explained her symptoms: headache, cough and a lot of tiredness.

La doctora la escuchó y le miró la garganta y los oídos.

The doctor listened to her and looked at her throat and ears.

«Es un resfriado fuerte, pero no es grave», dijo.

"It's a bad cold, but it isn't serious," she said.

Le recetó unas pastillas y le recomendó descansar.

She prescribed some pills and recommended that she rest.

«Bebe mucha agua, come fruta y duerme ocho horas», le aconsejó.

"Drink lots of water, eat fruit and sleep eight hours," she advised.

Ana fue a la farmacia con la receta.

Ana went to the pharmacy with the prescription.

El farmacéutico le dio la medicina y le deseó buena suerte.

The pharmacist gave her the medicine and wished her good luck.

Después de tres días, Ana se sintió mucho mejor.

After three days, Ana felt much better.

Ahora cuida su salud: camina, descansa y come más verduras.

Now she takes care of her health: she walks, rests and eats more vegetables.

⚠️ Plurals to note: el ojo → los ojos, la nariz → las narices, el pie → los pies, el corazón → los corazones.

2.2. doler: "to hurt"

doler works like gustar (Lesson 14): the thing that hurts is the subject.

me / te / le / nos / os / les + duele (una cosa) / duelen (varias cosas)
  • Me duele la cabeza.My head hurts. (literally: the head hurts to me)
  • Me duelen los pies.My feet hurt. (plural → duelen)
  • ¿Te duele la garganta?Does your throat hurt?
  • A Ana le dolía el estómago.Ana's stomach hurt.

You can also use a noun: tener dolor de + part of the body.

  • Tengo dolor de cabeza.I have a headache.
  • Tiene dolor de estómago.He has a stomach ache.

2.3. Being ill: tener y estar

  • tener fiebre / tos / un resfriado / la gripeto have a fever / a cough / a cold / the flu
  • estar enfermo / enfermato be ill
  • estar mal / sentirse malto feel bad
  • Ahora está sana otra vez.Now she is well again.
💡 Spain vs Latin America: in Spain people often say estar malo / mala to mean "to be ill" (Mi hermano está malo). In Latin America estar enfermo is more common. Both are understood everywhere.

2.4. Giving advice

To recommend something, use deber (should / must) or tener que (have to) + infinitive.

  • Debes descansar.You should rest.
  • Tienes que beber mucha agua.You have to drink lots of water.
  • No debes tomar tanto café.You shouldn't drink so much coffee.
  • Es mejor dormir ocho horas.It's better to sleep eight hours.
💡 Preview (advanced): the doctor in the passage said Bebe agua, Come fruta, Duerme ocho horas. These are commands, or imperativo. You will study them properly in Lesson 24; for now, just recognise them as advice.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences for the doctor's office. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Say what hurts:

Me duele la cabeza y me duelen los pies.

My head hurts and my feet hurt.

2. Describe a symptom:

Tengo tos y un poco de fiebre.

I have a cough and a slight fever.

3. Ask for help:

¿Puede darme algo para el dolor?

Can you give me something for the pain?

4. Give advice:

Debes descansar y beber mucha agua.

You should rest and drink lots of water.

5. Reassure someone:

No es grave; en unos días estarás bien.

It's not serious; in a few days you'll be fine.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two friends talk after one of them was ill.

Marco: ¡Hola, Julia! ¿Cómo estás? Ayer no viniste a clase.

Marco: Hi, Julia! How are you? You didn't come to class yesterday.

Julia: Estaba enferma. Me dolía la garganta y tenía fiebre.

Julia: I was ill. My throat hurt and I had a fever.

Marco: ¿Y fuiste al médico?

Marco: And did you go to the doctor?

Julia: Sí. Me recetó unas pastillas y me dijo que descansara.

Julia: Yes. She prescribed me some pills and told me to rest.

Marco: ¿Y ahora cómo te sientes?

Marco: And how do you feel now?

Julia: Mucho mejor, gracias. Solo tengo un poco de tos.

Julia: Much better, thanks. I only have a bit of a cough.

Marco: Debes cuidarte. Come fruta y duerme bien.

Marco: You should take care of yourself. Eat fruit and sleep well.

New words in the dialogue: viniste (you came), que descansara (to rest — advanced subjunctive), solo (only).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • Body: cabeza, ojo, nariz, boca, oreja, cuello, brazo, dedo, pierna, pie, espalda, estómago, garganta, pecho, corazón.
  • doler works like gustar: me duele la cabeza / me duelen los pies.
  • Illness: tener fiebre / tos / un resfriado; estar enfermo.
  • Advice: deber + infinitivo, tener que + infinitivo.
  • Spanish uses the article: la cabeza, not mi cabeza.

5.2. Further reading — a healthy routine

Context: A short blog post about staying healthy. Read it quietly:

Para estar sano, no necesitas cosas complicadas.

To be healthy, you don't need complicated things.

Duerme ocho horas y bebe agua durante el día.

Sleep eight hours and drink water during the day.

Come fruta y verduras; no comas mucha comida rápida.

Eat fruit and vegetables; don't eat much fast food.

Camina media hora cada día y haz algo de deporte.

Walk half an hour every day and do some sport.

Si te duele algo por muchos días, ve al médico.

If something hurts for many days, go to the doctor.

New words: complicado (complicated), media hora (half an hour), comida rápida (fast food), algo (something).
