LESSON 54 — CINEMA & TELEVISION

Lesson goals:
  • Read about three directors who took Spanish-language film global: Almodóvar, del Toro, Cuarón — plus the telenovela.
  • Recycle si-clauses (real and unreal) and the conditional.
  • Recycle verb periphrases: volver a, ponerse a, seguir + gerundio.
  • Pick up words: pantalla, rodaje, premio, protagonista, banda sonora, doblaje + por consiguiente.

Core vocabulary: pantalla, rodaje, director, actor, actriz, plano, secuencia, montaje, doblaje, subtítulo, banda sonora, premio, nominación, galardón, palmarés, festival, alfombra roja, protagonista, antagonista, interpretación, melodrama, terror, fantasía, suspenso, cortometraje, largometraje, ópera prima, precuela, remake, platea, taquillazo, puerta, telenovela, mixteco, por consiguiente
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): cameo, tráiler, spoiler

1. READING PASSAGE (TRES DIRECTORES, TRES MUNDOS)

Context: How Spanish-language cinema went from local screens to the Oscars. Read each line; the English below it is only a check.

(Conditions and wishes run through film talk — if… and would… are today's grammar.)

En los años ochenta, Pedro Almodóvar hacía cine en Madrid con amigos, sin dinero y sin ayuda, mientras La Movida era la fiesta de música, color y noches sin fin.

In the eighties, Pedro Almodóvar made films in Madrid with friends, with no money and no help, while La Movida was the party of music, colour and endless nights.

Sus melodramas de colores fuertes ponen a las mujeres en el centro: madres, amigas y vecinas que hablan sin parar, y si las ves una vez, las recuerdas siempre.

His brightly coloured melodramas put women at the centre: mothers, friends and neighbours who talk non-stop — and if you see them once, you remember them forever.

Su película Mujeres al borde de un ataque de nervios lo hizo conocido en todo el mundo y le dio su primer gran premio, y con Todo sobre mi madre ganó el Óscar.

His film Women on the Verge of a Nervous Breakdown made him known worldwide and won him his first big prize — and with All About My Mother he won the Oscar.

Con Todo sobre mi madre ganó el Óscar, y con Hable con ella ganó otro por escribir.

With All About My Mother he won the Oscar, and with Talk to Her he won another for writing.

Con Hable con ella ganó otro por escribir, porque sus madres hablan mejor que nadie.

With Talk to Her he won another for writing, because his mothers speak better than anyone.

En El laberinto del fauno, una niña encuentra un mundo de fantasía en medio de la guerra, y la película mezcla terror y cuento donde el fauno no es lo más peligroso.

In Pan's Labyrinth, a girl finds a fantasy world in the middle of war, and the film mixes horror and fairy tale where the faun is not the most dangerous thing.

Lo más peligroso es la guerra de los hombres, y con La forma del agua, Guillermo del Toro ganó el Óscar a mejor director y a mejor película, un premio que celebra la fantasía y el terror.

The most dangerous thing is the war of men — and with The Shape of Water, Guillermo del Toro won the Oscar for best director and best picture, a prize that celebrates fantasy and horror.

Cuarón cuenta el espacio y la memoria: Gravity pasa en el espacio y Roma en su barrio de niño, dos mundos distintos con un director solo.

Cuarón tells of space and memory: Gravity takes place in space and Roma in his childhood neighbourhood — two different worlds with one director alone.

Roma es en blanco y negro, se habla mixteco y su protagonista era una joven sin experiencia: Yalitza Aparicio, que trabajaba como maestra y habla mixteco con una interpretación inolvidable.

Roma is in black and white, Mixtec is spoken, and its lead was a young woman with no experience: Yalitza Aparicio, who worked as a teacher and speaks Mixtec with an unforgettable performance.

Tuvo una nominación al Óscar y abrió la puerta del cine a las lenguas de México.

It earned an Oscar nomination and opened the door of cinema to Mexico's languages.

En la pantalla pequeña, las telenovelas de México y Colombia se veían en cien países, y mi abuela las veía todas las tardes.

On the small screen, Mexican and Colombian soap operas were watched in a hundred countries — and my grandma watched them every afternoon.

Hoy las series en español viajan por el mundo: La casa de papel se vio en todos los países, y si no la has visto, la verás pronto.

Today Spanish-language series travel the world: Money Heist was watched in every country — and if you haven't seen it, you'll see it soon.

Me gustaría verla en pantalla grande con subtítulos, porque sería una pena verla doblada.

I'd like to see it on the big screen with subtitles, because it would be a shame to see it dubbed.

El rodaje dura meses, la platea aplaude diez minutos y el taquillazo llega después.

Filming lasts months, the audience applauds for ten minutes and the box-office hit comes after.

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. Si-clauses: real and unreal (recycled from L39)

Type 1 (possible): si + present → future/imperative. Type 2 (dream): si + imperfect subjunctive → conditional. Never put the conditional after si.

si + present → future  ·  si + imperfect subjunctive → conditional
  • Si la ves en versión original, lees los subtítulos.If you watch it in the original version, you read the subtitles. (type 1)
  • Si ganara el Óscar, hablaría de su madre.If he won the Oscar, he would talk about his mother. (type 2)
  • Si tuviera su cámara, empezaría mañana mismo.If I had his camera, I would start tomorrow.

2.2. Periphrases on set (recycled from L44)

  • Después de Gravity, volvió a México y contó su infancia.After Gravity, he went back to Mexico and told his childhood. (volver a = again)
  • La sala se puso a aplaudir antes del final.The audience burst into applause before the end. (ponerse a = suddenly start)
  • Sigue viviendo entre Madrid y México.He still lives between Madrid and Mexico. (seguir + gerundio = still)
  • Quiere realismo; por consiguiente, trabaja con actores nuevos.He wants realism; therefore he works with new actors.

2.3. The conditional for wishes (recycled from L39)

  • Me gustaría verla en pantalla grande.I'd like to see it on the big screen.
  • Sería una pena verla doblada.It would be a shame to see it dubbed.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences from the cinema. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. The shoot:

El rodaje de Roma duró nueve semanas en la ciudad.

Filming Roma lasted nine weeks in the city.

2. The actress:

Sin ser actriz, dio una interpretación inolvidable.

Without being an actress, she gave an unforgettable performance.

3. The long take:

El plano más largo dura trece minutos y no tiene montaje.

The longest shot lasts thirteen minutes and has no editing.

4. Dubbing vs subtitles:

En España se prefiere el doblaje; en América, los subtítulos.

In Spain dubbing is preferred; in Latin America, subtitles.

5. The wish:

Sería una pena verla doblada.

It would be a shame to see it dubbed.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two friends come out of the cinema, arguing about the trailer, the music and the ending.

Paula: El tráiler no cuenta nada, pero la banda sonora ya me emocionó.

Paula: The trailer gives nothing away, but the soundtrack already moved me.

Jorge: ¿Viste el cameo?

Jorge: Did you see the cameo?

Jorge: Sale el director un minuto, con sombrero.

Jorge: The director appears for a minute, wearing a hat.

Paula: La banda sonora mezcla canciones y ruidos de calle. Eso es Madrid puro.

Paula: The soundtrack mixes songs and street noise. That's pure Madrid.

Jorge: Si ganara el Óscar, hablaría de su madre, seguro.

Jorge: If he won the Oscar, he'd talk about his mother, for sure.

Paula: No me hagas spoilers del final. La vemos otra vez el sábado.

Paula: Don't spoil the ending for me. We'll watch it again on Saturday.

New words in the dialogue: cameo, tráiler, spoiler — the three words every viewer needs.

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • Si type 1: si la ves…, lees… — possible condition.
  • Si type 2: si ganara…, hablaría… — dream condition; never a conditional after si.
  • Periphrases: volvió a México (again), se puso a aplaudir (sudden start), sigue viviendo (still).
  • Three directors: Almodóvar films women and melodrama; del Toro films monsters and fairy tales; Cuarón films space and memory — and television now speaks Spanish worldwide.

5.2. Further reading — los premios y el público

Context: A short note on prizes, audiences and first films. Read it quietly:

Su palmarés tiene dos Óscar y un Goya, y cada festival le da un galardón nuevo.

His track record holds two Oscars and a Goya, and every festival gives him a new award.

El antagonista no es un hombre, es la guerra: por eso sus historias de suspenso no dejan dormir.

The antagonist is not a man; it is war: that is why his suspense stories don't let you sleep.

Empezó con cortometrajes y su ópera prima llegó a los treinta años.

He started with short films, and his debut feature came at thirty.

Hollywood pide una precuela; él dice que no, y la platea aplaude diez minutos.

Hollywood asks for a prequel; he says no, and the audience applauds for ten minutes.

No necesita un remake en inglés: la película ya fue un taquillazo en español.

He needs no English remake: the film was already a box-office hit in Spanish.

New words: palmarés (track record), ópera prima (debut), taquillazo (box-office hit) — from today's table.
