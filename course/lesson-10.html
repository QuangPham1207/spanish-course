LESSON 10 — STEM-CHANGING VERBS

Lesson goals:
  • Understand the three stem changes: e→ie, o→ue and e→i.
  • Use key verbs: querer, poder, pedir, pensar, volver, jugar.
  • See why nosotros / vosotros don't change (the "boot").
  • Learn ir (to go) and tener que (to have to).

Core vocabulary: poder, pedir, jugar, ir, volver, pensar, empezar, cerrar, entender, preferir, servir, repetir, encontrar, tener que, lo siento, tenis, fútbol
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): practicar, información, deporte, partido, venir, casi, sin, ¡qué pena!, a menudo

1. READING PASSAGE (PLANS AND HABITS)

Context: Kenji writes about studying, sport and making plans. Read each line; the English below it is only a check.

💡 Memory hook: Stem-changing verbs are also called "boot verbs": the change happens in every form except nosotros and vosotros — the shape on a conjugation chart looks like a boot.

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. What changes (and what doesn't)

In these verbs the stressed vowel of the stem changes in the present. Take pensar (to think): the e becomes ie in every form where you hear the stress on the stem — but nosotros / vosotros keep the original vowel:

pensar → pienso · piensas · piensa · pensamos · pensáis · piensan
⚠️ This is why querer (Lesson 07) has quiero but queremos. The change is not irregular — it is a regular pattern.

2.5. ir (to go) and tener que (to have to)

ir is very irregular but essential. Use ir a + a place:

PersonirEnglish
yovoyI go
túvasyou go

nosotrosvamoswe go

  • ¿Vas a la fiesta?Are you going to the party?

💡 ir a + infinitive also builds the near future: Voy a comer = "I'm going to eat" (Lesson 21).

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences you can use from day one. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Say you can or can't:

Hoy no puedo, pero mañana puedo.

Today I can't, but tomorrow I can.

2. Order something:

Pido una sopa y una ensalada.

I'll have a soup and a salad.

3. Talk about sport:

Jugamos al tenis los sábados.

We play tennis on Saturdays.

4. Say you have to do something:

Lo siento, tengo que trabajar.

I'm sorry, I have to work.

5. Go somewhere:

Voy al centro y vuelvo a las seis.

I'm going to the centre and I'll be back at six.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two friends make plans to play football.

Ana: ¿Puedes jugar al fútbol el sábado?

Ana: Can you play football on Saturday?

Kenji: Sí, puedo. ¿A qué hora empieza el partido?

Kenji: Yes, I can. What time does the match start?

Ana: A las seis. ¿Quieres venir con nosotros?

Ana: At six. Do you want to come with us?

Kenji: Claro. ¿Y Lucía?

Kenji: Sure. And Lucía?

Ana: Ella no puede. Tiene que estudiar.

Ana: She can't. She has to study.

Kenji: ¡Qué pena! Bueno, jugamos sin ella.

Kenji: What a shame! Well, we'll play without her.

New words in the dialogue: partido (match, game), claro (sure, of course), sin (without), ¡Qué pena! (What a shame!).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • Stem-changing verbs change their stem vowel except in nosotros / vosotros.
  • e→ie: quiero, pienso, empiezo, entiendo, cierro, prefiero.
  • o→ue: puedo, vuelvo, duermo, encuentro, cuesta; u→ue: juego.
  • e→i: pido, sirvo, repito.
  • ir: voy, vas, va, vamos, vais, van; tener que + infinitive = to have to.

5.2. Further reading — plans for the weekend

Context: A short note about a busy weekend. Read it quietly:

Este fin de semana quiero descansar, pero tengo que trabajar el sábado.

This weekend I want to rest, but I have to work on Saturday.

Por la mañana voy a la oficina y por la tarde juego al fútbol con mis amigos.

In the morning I go to the office and in the afternoon I play football with my friends.

El domingo pienso visitar a mi familia.

On Sunday I'm planning to visit my family.

Mi hermana prefiere quedarse en casa y leer.

My sister prefers to stay at home and read.

¿Y tú? ¿Puedes venir a cenar el domingo?

And you? Can you come to dinner on Sunday?

New words: fin de semana (weekend), descansar (to rest), visitar (to visit), quedarse (to stay), a menudo (often).
