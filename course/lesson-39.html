LESSON 39 — THE CONDITIONAL & SI-CLAUSES

Lesson goals:
  • Use the conditional (haría, podría, me gustaría) for hypothetical actions, polite requests and advice.
  • Build real conditions with si + present (type 1): si aceptas, vivirás….
  • Build unreal conditions with si + imperfect subjunctive (type 2): si tuviera…, aceptaría….
  • Read about a life decision: a scholarship abroad.
  • Pick up words: beca, préstamo, deuda, alquiler, mudanza, arriesgar, arrepentirse.

Core vocabulary: haría, podrías, me gustaría, debería, sería, tendrías, beca, préstamo, deuda, alquiler, mudanza, papeleo, visado, extranjero/a, valentía, indecisión, indeciso/a, supuesto/a, consecuencia, hipótesis, motivo, plazo, merecer, arriesgar, arriesgado/a, arrepentirse, atreverse, costear, emprender, suponer, merecido/a, favor
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): imagínate, cobardía, jubilarse

1. READING PASSAGE (UNA BECA EN EL EXTRANJERO)

Context: Lucía, an architect in Valencia, has been offered a scholarship to study in Bogotá. She turns the decision over in her mind.

(Watch the verb forms: si + present for real possibilities, the conditional for imagined results, and si + tuviera for dreams she is less sure about.)

A Lucía le han ofrecido una beca para estudiar urbanismo en Bogotá.

Lucía has been offered a scholarship to study urban planning in Bogotá.

Si acepta la beca, vivirá dos años en el extranjero.

If she accepts the scholarship, she will live abroad for two years.

El programa le costearía los estudios, pero ella pagaría el alquiler y la mudanza.

The programme would cover her studies, but she would pay the rent and the move.

Si tuviera que pedir un préstamo, la deuda sería enorme.

If she had to take out a loan, the debt would be huge.

Su madre dice que debería aceptar y que el papeleo del visado no supone ningún problema.

Her mother says she should accept and that the visa paperwork is no problem.

Lucía se pregunta si se arrepentiría de rechazar algo así.

Lucía wonders whether she would regret turning down something like this.

En el supuesto más valiente, acepta, se atreve y emprende el viaje sin mirar atrás.

In the bravest scenario, she accepts, dares, and starts the journey without looking back.

Pero la indecisión no la deja dormir: ¿y si no merece la pena arriesgar tanto?

But indecision keeps her awake: what if risking so much isn't worth it?

Al final, la consecuencia que más teme no es fracasar, sino preguntarse toda la vida qué habría pasado.

In the end, the consequence she fears most is not failing, but wondering all her life what would have happened.

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. The conditional: what it means

The conditional is the "would" tense: hypothetical results (pagaría), polite requests (¿podrías…?), wishes (me gustaría) and advice (deberías). It is formed from the infinitive + -ía endings (hablar → hablaría), with a few irregular stems (tener → tendr-, poder → podr-, hacer → har-, decir → dir-, salir → saldr-, venir → vendr-).

infinitive + -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían  ·  tendría, podría, haría, diría, saldría, vendría
  • El programa me costearía los estudios.The programme would cover my studies.
  • ¿Podrías ayudarme con el papeleo?Could you help me with the paperwork? (polite)
  • Me gustaría vivir en el extranjero.I would like to live abroad.
  • Deberías aceptar la beca.You should accept the scholarship. (advice)
💡 Spain-vs-LatAm note: the conditional works identically everywhere. In requests, Latin America often prefers ¿puedes…? where Spain uses ¿podrías…? — both are correct.

2.2. Real conditions (type 1): si + present

When the condition is a real possibility, use si + present indicative, and the result in the future, imperative or present.

si + present  →  future / imperative / present
  • Si acepta la beca, vivirá en Bogotá.If she accepts the scholarship, she will live in Bogotá.
  • Si necesitas dinero, pide un préstamo.If you need money, ask for a loan.
  • Si el plazo termina hoy, tenemos que decidir ya.If the deadline ends today, we have to decide now.
⚠️ Common mistake: never put the future or conditional directly after si. Say si acepta, not si aceptará / si aceptaría.

2.3. Unreal conditions (type 2): si + imperfect subjunctive

When the condition is imagined, unlikely or contrary to fact, use si + imperfect subjunctive (tuviera, fuera, pudiera) and the result in the conditional. You only need to recognise the -ra forms here; the full paradigm comes in Lesson 57 (advanced).

si + imperfect subjunctive (-ra)  →  conditional (-ía)
  • Si tuviera una beca, me atrevería a mudarme.If I had a scholarship, I would dare to move.
  • Si fuera más valiente, emprendería el viaje.If she were braver, she would start the journey.
  • Si no supusiera tanta deuda, aceptaría mañana.If it didn't mean so much debt, she would accept tomorrow.
  • ¿Qué harías tú en su lugar?What would you do in her place?

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences with the conditional and si. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. A polite wish:

Me gustaría vivir en el extranjero.

I would like to live abroad.

2. Advice:

Deberías aceptar la beca.

You should accept the scholarship.

3. A real condition:

Si acepta la beca, vivirá dos años fuera.

If she accepts the scholarship, she will live away for two years.

4. An unreal condition:

Si tuviera una beca, me atrevería a mudarme.

If I had a scholarship, I would dare to move.

5. Regret imagined:

Se arrepentiría de rechazar algo así.

She would regret turning down something like this.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Lucía asks her brother for advice.

Lucía: Si acepto, tendría que pedir un préstamo.

Lucía: If I accept, I would have to take out a loan.

Hermano: Si yo fuera tú, no me preocuparía por la deuda.

Brother: If I were you, I wouldn't worry about the debt.

Lucía: ¿Y si no mereciera la pena?

Lucía: And what if it weren't worth it?

Hermano: Imagínate dentro de diez años: ¿qué te arrepentirías más de no haber intentado?

Brother: Imagine yourself in ten years: what would you most regret not having tried?

Lucía: Tienes razón. Pediré otro plazo y prepararé el papeleo.

Lucía: You're right. I'll ask for another deadline and prepare the paperwork.

New words in the dialogue: valentía is implied; imagínate (imagine) and plazo (deadline) reappear here.

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • Conditional = infinitive + -ía: hablaría, tendría, podría. Uses: hypothetical results, polite requests, wishes, advice.
  • Type 1 (real): si + present → future. Never put the future after si.
  • Type 2 (unreal): si + imperfect subjunctive (-ra) → conditional.
  • Type 3 (si hubiera… habría…) comes in Lesson 58 (advanced).

5.2. Further reading — el motivo de Marcos

Context: Another young professional faces the same question. Read it quietly:

Si Marcos aceptara el puesto en Lima, costearía su mudanza con sus ahorros.

If Marcos accepted the post in Lima, he would cover his move with his savings.

No quiere pedir ningún favor ni acumular más deudas.

He doesn't want to ask any favours or pile up more debts.

Su motivo es claro: un trabajo merecido después de años de esfuerzo.

His motive is clear: a well-earned job after years of effort.

Si el plazo se acaba, emprenderá el viaje con lo puesto.

If the deadline runs out, he will start the journey with just the clothes on his back.

Sería una decisión arriesgada, pero la indecisión sería peor.

It would be a risky decision, but indecision would be worse.

New words: acumular (to accumulate) appears here in passing.
