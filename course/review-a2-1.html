READING PASSAGE (EL VIAJE DE MARTA A BUENOS AIRES)

Context: Marta tells the story of her first trip to Argentina. The imperfect paints the scene, the preterite tells what happened, and the present perfect links it all to now.

(Read top to bottom — let the Spanish carry you. The English is only a check.)

El año pasado Marta hizo su primer viaje a Argentina.

Last year Marta took her first trip to Argentina.

Era septiembre y en Buenos Aires empezaba la primavera.

It was September and spring was beginning in Buenos Aires.

Cuando llegó al aeropuerto, estaba cansada pero muy contenta.

When she arrived at the airport, she was tired but very happy.

Al principio no entendía el acento de la gente, pero poco a poco lo comprendió.

At first she didn't understand people's accent, but little by little she understood it.

El primer día caminó por el centro y visitó la Casa Rosada.

On the first day she walked around the centre and visited the Casa Rosada.

Mientras paseaba por el barrio de La Boca, un hombre le contó la historia del tango.

While she was strolling through the La Boca neighbourhood, a man told her the history of tango.

De repente, empezó a llover y ella entró en un café.

Suddenly, it started to rain and she went into a café.

Allí conoció a Diego, un músico argentino que tocaba la guitarra.

There she met Diego, an Argentine musician who played the guitar.

Hablaron durante horas y él le enseñó algunos pasos de tango.

They talked for hours and he taught her a few tango steps.

Esa noche bailaron en una milonga hasta las tres de la mañana.

That night they danced in a milonga until three in the morning.

Los días siguientes visitaron juntos un mercado y comieron asado.

On the following days they visited a market together and ate asado.

Marta dice: «Nunca he conocido a una persona tan amable».

Marta says: "I have never met such a kind person."

Ha vuelto a Argentina dos veces desde entonces.

She has returned to Argentina twice since then.

Para ella, ha sido el mejor viaje de su vida.

For her, it has been the best trip of her life.

💡 Notice how it all fits together: background in the imperfect (era, empezaba, estaba, paseaba, tocaba), events in the preterite (hizo, llegó, contó, conoció, bailaron, comieron), and the experience in the present perfect (he conocido, ha vuelto, ha sido).

THE SENTENCES, BROKEN INTO PATTERNS

Re-read these mini-groups. Each one is a pattern you can reuse forever.

The preterite: finished events

  • Caminó por el centro y visitó la Casa Rosada.She walked around the centre and visited the Casa Rosada.
  • Conoció a Diego, un músico que tocaba la guitarra.She met Diego, a musician who played the guitar.
  • Hicieron un viaje inolvidable.They took an unforgettable trip.

The imperfect: the scene and habits

  • Era septiembre y empezaba la primavera.It was September and spring was beginning.
  • Estaba cansada pero muy contenta.She was tired but very happy.
  • Mientras paseaba, alguien le contaba una historia.While she was strolling, someone was telling her a story.

Connectors that move a story

  • Al principio no entendía… pero poco a poco lo comprendió.At first she didn't understand… but little by little she understood.
  • De repente, empezó a llover.Suddenly, it started to rain.
  • Los días siguientes visitaron juntos un mercado.On the following days they visited a market together.

The present perfect: experience and results

  • Nunca he conocido a una persona tan amable.I have never met such a kind person.
  • Ha vuelto a Argentina dos veces desde entonces.She has returned to Argentina twice since then.
  • Ha sido el mejor viaje de su vida.It has been the best trip of her life.
⚠️ One last reminder: the only thing a review asks is that you read. No writing, no exercises, no memorising. If a sentence felt easy today, that is progress.
