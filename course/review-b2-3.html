READING 1 — THE VOTE AND THE MARKET (URNA Y TANDA)

Context: A chronicle from election Sunday: the polling station in the morning, the market at noon, the mountains beyond. Read it from top to bottom — the English below is only a check.

Si bien la urna abrió a las nueve, doña Rosa votó a las siete: primero la tanda, después la papeleta.

Although the ballot box opened at nine, doña Rosa voted at seven: first the tanda, then the ballot.

El escrutinio fue limpio, mesa por mesa; sin embargo, la abstención sigue doliendo en los barrios.

The count was clean, table by table; however, abstention keeps hurting in the neighbourhoods.

La inflación sigue subiendo, el fiado engorda, y por consiguiente la caravana sigue caminando al norte.

Inflation keeps rising, tick grows, and consequently the caravan keeps walking north.

Terminada la jornada, el glaciar seguía allí arriba; abajo, la minga plantaba el huerto.

With the day over, the glacier was still up there; below, the minga planted the garden.

Se registra un metro menos de hielo; se prevé más calor; el ayllu exige la consulta previa.

One metre less of ice is recorded; more heat is forecast; the ayllu demands prior consultation.

En cambio, la buena noticia cabe en una canoa: la cadena de frío llegó, y se vacunó todo el pueblo.

On the other hand, the good news fits in a canoe: the cold chain arrived, and the whole village was vaccinated.

READING 2 — THE SCREEN AND THE STREET (BULO Y MAREA)

Context: A second feature, on the other half of the module — lies, marches, labs and bodegas. Read it quietly:

El bulo sería de 2019, según los verificadores; se dice que lo resucitan en cada elección.

The hoax is allegedly from 2019, according to the checkers; it is said they revive it every election.

La burbuja repite, la portada desmiente, y la tertulia discute la pauta hasta la madrugada.

The bubble repeats, the front page debunks, and the talk show debates the ad money until dawn.

Exigen que el consentimiento sea ley y que los cuidados se repartan: la marea verde no se rinde.

They demand consent become law and care be shared: the green wave doesn't give up.

La paridad avanza, la brecha resiste; no solo marchan, sino que gobiernan: hay ministras.

Parity advances, the gap resists; they not only march, but govern: there are women ministers.

El telescopio abrirá su ojo; la alerta sísmica dará sesenta segundos; la matrona calmará al bebé.

The telescope will open its eye; the seismic alert will give sixty seconds; the midwife will calm the baby.

Y en Queens, la bodega donde desayunan huele a café: «che», «vale», «órale» — la diáspora ya es casa.

And in Queens, the bodega where they breakfast smells of coffee: "che", "vale", "órale" — the diaspora is home now.

De la urna a la bodega: del desarraigo al arraigo, la pertenencia se practica.

From the ballot box to the bodega: from uprooting to rootedness, belonging is practised.

💡 Register note: a contemporary feature mixes everything at once — news passive (fue limpio, se vacunó), trends (sigue subiendo), rumour (sería, se dice que), demands (exigen que sea) and belonging (donde desayunan). You are now reading all five at once.

THE SENTENCES, BROKEN INTO PATTERNS

Re-read these mini-groups. Each one is a pattern you can reuse forever.

News passive (L73)

  • El escrutinio fue limpio, mesa por mesa.The count was clean, table by table.
  • Será investido solo si los pactos aguantan.He will be invested only if the pacts hold.

Trends that never stop (L74)

  • La inflación sigue subiendo; la caravana sigue caminando.Inflation keeps rising; the caravan keeps walking.
  • Llevan años encadenando contratos de días.They have spent years chaining day-long contracts.

Absolute openers & scientific se (L75)

  • Terminada la jornada, la minga planta el huerto.With the day over, the minga plants the garden.
  • Se registra un metro menos; se prevé más calor.One metre less is recorded; more heat is forecast.

Rumour conditional (L76)

  • El bulo sería de 2019; habría nacido en una granja.The hoax is allegedly from 2019; it supposedly originated on a farm.
  • Según los verificadores, lo resucitan siempre.According to the checkers, they always revive it.

Demands (L77)

  • Exigen que el consentimiento sea ley.They demand consent become law.
  • Luchan para que ningún micromachismo pase de largo.They fight so no small machismo slips by.

Protocols & hypotheses (L78)

  • Se vacunó todo el pueblo; se rastrearon los contactos.The whole village was vaccinated; contacts were traced.
  • El desierto será la capital del cielo.The desert will be the capital of the sky.

Belonging clauses (L79)

  • La bodega donde desayunan huele a café.The bodega where they breakfast smells of coffee.
  • Che, vale, órale: todo el mapa en el recreo.Che, vale, órale: the whole map in the playground.

Correlatives (L80)

  • No solo marchan, sino que gobiernan.They not only march, but govern.
  • Tanto en la urna como en la tanda.Both at the ballot box and in the tanda.
⚠️ One last reminder: the only thing a review asks is that you read. No writing, no exercises, no memorising. If a sentence felt easy today, that is progress — and watch the verbs: facts take the indicative (fue limpio, sigue subiendo), wishes and doubts take the subjunctive (exigen que sea, para que pase), rumours take the conditional (sería).
