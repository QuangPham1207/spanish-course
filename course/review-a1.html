REVIEW A1 — GETTING AROUND THE LEVEL

This is a re-reading lesson, not a test. There are no exercises and nothing to send. Just read and enjoy how much of Level A1 you now recognise.

What this review re-reads:
  • Lessons 01-05 — greetings, introductions, numbers, family, nouns.
  • Lessons 06-10 — the present tense, food, routine, shopping, stem-changing verbs.
  • Lessons 11-15 — the city, transport, weather, hobbies and a consolidation reading.

Almost every word here comes from the A1 lessons. A few extra, very common words appear in context (listed after the passage). No need to memorise anything — re-reading is exactly how it settles in.

READING PASSAGE (READ TWICE IF YOU LIKE)

Context: First a dialogue between two new friends, then a short text about one of their days.

(Read top to bottom — let the Spanish carry you. The English is only a check.)

Diego: ¡Buenos días! Me llamo Diego. ¿Y tú?

Diego: Good morning! My name is Diego. And you?

Ana: Hola, soy Ana. Encantada. ¿De dónde eres?

Ana: Hi, I'm Ana. Nice to meet you. Where are you from?

Diego: Soy de Salamanca. Vivo en un piso cerca de la plaza mayor. ¿Y tú?

Diego: I'm from Salamanca. I live in a flat near the main square. And you?

Ana: Yo soy de Valencia, pero ahora trabajo en Madrid.

Ana: I'm from Valencia, but right now I work in Madrid.

Diego: ¿Qué te gusta hacer en tu tiempo libre?

Diego: What do you like to do in your free time?

Ana: Me gusta leer y nadar. También me encanta la música.

Ana: I like reading and swimming. I also love music.

Diego: ¿Qué tiempo hace en Valencia?

Diego: What's the weather like in Valencia?

Ana: En invierno no hace mucho frío y en verano hace sol. El clima es suave.

Ana: In winter it isn't very cold and in summer it's sunny. The climate is mild.

Diego: ¿Quieres ir al museo el sábado?

Diego: Do you want to go to the museum on Saturday?

Ana: Claro. Podemos ir en metro; la estación está cerca de mi casa.

Ana: Sure. We can go by metro; the station is near my house.

Diego: Perfecto. Después podemos comer en un restaurante del centro.

Diego: Perfect. Afterwards we can eat at a restaurant in the centre.

Ana: De acuerdo. ¡Hasta el sábado!

Ana: OK. See you on Saturday!

The short text:

Por la mañana, Diego se levanta a las ocho y desayuna café con pan.

In the morning, Diego gets up at eight and has coffee with bread for breakfast.

Va a clase en autobús o camina si hace sol.

He goes to class by bus or walks if it's sunny.

Su barrio es tranquilo: hay un mercado, un parque y muchos cafés.

His neighbourhood is quiet: there's a market, a park and many cafés.

Por la tarde, si no llueve, juega al fútbol con sus amigos en el parque.

In the afternoon, if it isn't raining, he plays football with his friends in the park.

A Diego le encanta la comida española: la tortilla, el jamón y las tapas.

Diego loves Spanish food: tortilla, ham and tapas.

Por la noche vuelve a casa, cena con su familia y lee un libro.

At night he comes back home, has dinner with his family and reads a book.

Mañana es domingo, así que quiere ir a la playa.

Tomorrow is Sunday, so he wants to go to the beach.

THE SENTENCES, BROKEN INTO PATTERNS

Re-read these mini-groups. Each one is a pattern you can reuse forever.

Meeting and introducing yourself

  • ¡Buenos días! Me llamo Diego. ¿Y tú?Good morning! My name is Diego. And you?
  • Soy de Salamanca. Vivo en un piso cerca del centro.I'm from Salamanca. I live in a flat near the centre.
  • Encantada. Mucho gusto.Nice to meet you. Pleased to meet you.

Talking about likes

  • Me gusta leer y nadar. / Me encanta la música.I like reading and swimming. / I love music.
  • ¿Qué te gusta hacer en tu tiempo libre?What do you like to do in your free time?

The city and transport

  • ¿Quieres ir al museo? — Podemos ir en metro.Do you want to go to the museum? — We can go by metro.
  • La estación está cerca de mi casa.The station is near my house.
  • Hay un mercado, un parque y muchos cafés.There's a market, a park and many cafés.

The weather

  • ¿Qué tiempo hace? — En verano hace sol.What's the weather like? — In summer it's sunny.
  • Si no llueve, juega al fútbol.If it isn't raining, he plays football.

The daily routine and free time

  • Se levanta a las ocho y desayuna café con pan.He gets up at eight and has coffee with bread.
  • Por la noche vuelve a casa y lee un libro.At night he comes back home and reads a book.
  • Mañana quiere ir a la playa.Tomorrow he wants to go to the beach.
⚠️ One last reminder: the only thing a review asks is that you read. No writing, no exercises, no memorising. If a sentence felt easy today, that is progress.
