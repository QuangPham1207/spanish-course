LESSON 81 — READING STRATEGIES: HOW TO READ THE REAL WORLD

Lesson goals:
  • Learn the four moves of the Reading Ladder: estrategia, contexto, cognado and a good diccionario.
  • practise guessing from context: adivinar, releer, hojear — without stopping at every word.
  • Meet the traitors: falsos amigos like éxito, actual, sensible.
  • Read your first ladder text: a morning at a Mexico City newsstand, fully glossed.

Core vocabulary: estrategia, contexto, cognado, falso amigo, adivinar, comprobar, releer, hojear, subrayar, diccionario, acepción, atascado, por encima, en diagonal, de un vistazo, vistazo, seguir adelante, consultar, entrada de diccionario (plus known words hipótesis, paciencia, rendirse)
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): filología, etimología, sigla, acrónimo

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. The four moves: skim, guess, check, dictionary

Real readers don't read word by word. They read por encima (over the top), then en diagonal (skimming), then they guess (adivinar) and check (comprobar). The dictionary is the last move, not the first.

  • Lee el titular por encima y formula una hipótesis.Read the headline over and form a hypothesis.
  • Adivina la palabra por el contexto y comprueba tu idea después.Guess the word from context and check your idea afterwards.
  • Si te quedas atascado, relee: no te rindas.If you get stuck, re-read: don't give up.
por encima → en diagonal → adivinar → comprobar → diccionario

2.2. Cognates and false friends

A cognado helps: electoral, cultural, hospital. A falso amigo betrays: éxito means success (not exit = salida), actual means current (not actual = real, verdadero), sensible means sensitive (not sensible = sensato).

  • El éxito actual del programa es evidente.The program's current success is evident.
  • Es un lector sensible y sensato: siente y piensa.He is a sensitive, sensible reader: he feels and thinks.
  • La entrada de diccionario trae tres acepciones: lee el ejemplo.The dictionary entry has three senses: read the example.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences to read once. No need to memorize.

1. The method:

Lee por encima, adivina por el contexto, comprueba después.

Read over, guess from context, check afterwards.

2. The stuck moment:

Si te quedas atascado, relee con paciencia.

If you get stuck, re-read patiently.

3. The traitors:

Cuidado con los falsos amigos: el contexto manda.

Beware false friends: context rules.

4. The dictionary:

El diccionario viene al final, no al principio.

The dictionary comes at the end, not at the start.

5. The ladder:

Sigue adelante: una página más cada día.

Keep going: one more page each day.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): read the five slowly once. They are the whole method.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Lucía teaches her cousin how to read the paper without fear. No questions — just read.

Lucía: No busques cada palabra. Lee el titular por encima: ¿de qué va?

Lucía: Don't look up every word. Read the headline over: what's it about?

Tomás: Creo que habla de un hospital… Hay muchos cognados.

Tomás: I think it's about a hospital… There are lots of cognates.

Lucía: Exacto. Ahora adivina esta frase y comprueba en la siguiente.

Lucía: Exactly. Now guess this sentence and check in the next one.

Lucía: Echa un vistazo a la foto antes de leer: la imagen también informa.

Lucía: Take a look at the photo before reading: the image informs too.

Tomás: Me quedé atascado aquí… ¿Releo?

Tomás: I got stuck here… Should I re-read?

Lucía: Sí, con paciencia. Y subraya solo tres palabras, no treinta.

Lucía: Yes, patiently. And underline only three words, not thirty.

Tomás: Vale. Sigo adelante: ¡una página más!

Tomás: OK. I keep going: one more page!

New words in the dialogue: none — every word comes from today's table or earlier lessons. Notice the imperatives of method (lee, adivina, comprueba, relee, subraya, sigue).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • Method: por encima → en diagonal → adivinar → comprobar → diccionario.
  • Helpers vs traitors: cognados (de un vistazo) vs falsos amigos (el contexto manda).
  • Stuck protocol: releer con paciencia, subrayar poco, seguir adelante.

5.2. Further reading — el quiosco de mañana

Context: Tomorrow's text waits at the same newsstand. Read it with the method:

Mañana hojearás el periódico con calma: titular, foto, primera frase.

Tomorrow you'll flip through the paper calmly: headline, photo, first sentence.

Formularás tu hipótesis en diez segundos y la comprobarás en el segundo párrafo.

You'll form your hypothesis in ten seconds and check it in the second paragraph.

Solo tres palabras subrayadas. Solo una acepción consultada. Y a seguir.

Only three words underlined. Only one sense looked up. And onward.

El lector que sigue adelante ya está en la escalera.

The reader who keeps going is already on the ladder.

All words are from today's table. Notice the future tense as a plan (hojearás, formularás, comprobarás) — you met it back in Lesson 21.
