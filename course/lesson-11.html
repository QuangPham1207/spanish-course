LESSON 11 — THE CITY & DIRECTIONS

Lesson goals:
  • Say what there is in a place with hay.
  • Say where things are with estar (está / están).
  • Give directions: girar, cruzar, seguir recto, a la derecha / a la izquierda.
  • Use prepositions of place: al lado de, enfrente de, entre, detrás de…

Core vocabulary: hay, calle, plaza, parque, barrio, iglesia, banco, museo, edificio, esquina, derecha, izquierda, recto, cruzar, girar, semáforo, cerca, lejos, aquí, allí, al lado de, enfrente de, entre, detrás de, delante de
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): acera, avenida, hospital, jardín, río, turista, farmacia, recorrer, puente, antiguo

1. READING PASSAGE (A WALK THROUGH THE CITY)

Context: Someone describes their neighbourhood and gives directions across town. Read each line; the English below it is only a check.

(Read top to bottom — the Spanish is enough to get the gist.)

Valencia es una ciudad bonita y se puede recorrer a pie.

Valencia is a beautiful city and you can go around it on foot.

Mi barrio está cerca de un parque grande y de la plaza del centro.

My neighbourhood is near a big park and the central square.

En mi barrio hay un mercado, una iglesia antigua, un banco y una farmacia.

In my neighbourhood there's a market, an old church, a bank and a pharmacy.

Para ir al museo, salgo de casa y voy recto por la calle Mayor.

To go to the museum, I leave home and go straight along Calle Mayor.

Después cruzo la plaza y giro a la derecha en el semáforo.

Then I cross the square and turn right at the traffic light.

El museo está al lado de la estación y enfrente de un edificio moderno.

The museum is next to the station and opposite a modern building.

La estación no está lejos de aquí; está a diez minutos a pie.

The station isn't far from here; it's ten minutes away on foot.

Si giro a la izquierda, encuentro el hospital y un jardín pequeño.

If I turn left, I find the hospital and a small garden.

El centro de la ciudad está entre el parque y el río.

The city centre is between the park and the river.

Aquí hay muchos turistas porque la comida es rica y la gente es muy simpática.

There are lots of tourists here because the food is tasty and the people are very friendly.

💡 Memory hook: Two verbs do most of the work in a city. hay says what exists; está / están says where something is. If you can point to it, use está.

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. hay — there is / there are

hay never changes. It introduces something new — you are saying it exists:

hay + un / una / unos / unas + noun
  • En mi barrio hay un parque.In my neighbourhood there's a park.
  • Hay dos bancos en la plaza.There are two banks in the square. (hay stays the same)
  • ¿Hay un museo aquí?Is there a museum here?

2.2. estar — to be (location)

Once you know something exists, use está (singular) or están (plural) to say where it is. Remember from Lesson 01: ser = identity, estar = state and location.

PersonestarEnglish
(él / ella) el museoestáit is (located)
(ellos) los museosestánthey are (located)
  • El museo está al lado de la estación.The museum is next to the station.
  • Los bancos están en el centro.The banks are in the centre.
  • ¿Dónde está la plaza?Where is the square?
⚠️ hay vs está: Hay un museo = there's a museum (new information). El museo está allí = the museum is there (we both know which museum).

2.3. Giving directions

Directions use the verb ir (Lesson 10) plus a few new verbs:

  • Ve recto por esta calle.Go straight along this street.
  • Cruza la plaza.Cross the square.
  • Gira a la derecha / a la izquierda.Turn right / left.
  • El museo está a la derecha.The museum is on the right.
💡 a pie = on foot: La estación está a diez minutos a pie = The station is ten minutes away on foot.

2.4. Prepositions of place

These little phrases tell you exactly where something is. The ending changes with the noun:

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences for finding your way. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Ask where something is:

¿Dónde está el museo?

Where's the museum?

2. Say what's nearby:

Aquí hay un banco y una farmacia.

There's a bank and a pharmacy here.

3. Give a simple direction:

Gira a la derecha y cruza la plaza.

Turn right and cross the square.

4. Say how far it is:

La estación está cerca, a cinco minutos a pie.

The station is near, five minutes away on foot.

5. Describe a location:

El museo está al lado de la iglesia.

The museum is next to the church.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: A tourist asks a local for directions.

Turista: Perdón, ¿dónde está el museo?

Tourist: Excuse me, where's the museum?

Local: Está cerca. Ve recto por esta calle.

Local: It's near. Go straight along this street.

Turista: ¿Y después?

Tourist: And then?

Local: Cruza la plaza y gira a la izquierda en el semáforo.

Local: Cross the square and turn left at the traffic light.

Turista: ¿El museo está al lado de la iglesia?

Tourist: Is the museum next to the church?

Local: Sí, exacto. Está enfrente de un edificio moderno.

Local: Yes, exactly. It's opposite a modern building.

Turista: Muchas gracias. ¡Muy amable!

Tourist: Many thanks. Very kind!

New words in the dialogue: perdón (excuse me, sorry), exacto (exactly), amable (kind).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • hay = there is / there are (never changes).
  • está / están = is / are located.
  • Directions: ve recto, cruza, gira a la derecha / a la izquierda.
  • Prepositions: al lado de, enfrente de, delante de, detrás de, entre, cerca de, lejos de.
  • de + el = del: al lado del banco.

5.2. Further reading — my neighbourhood

Context: A short note describing a neighbourhood. Read it quietly:

Vivo en un barrio tranquilo, cerca del centro de la ciudad.

I live in a quiet neighbourhood, near the city centre.

En mi calle hay una panadería, un banco y una farmacia.

On my street there's a bakery, a bank and a pharmacy.

La plaza está al lado de la iglesia y enfrente de un parque.

The square is next to the church and opposite a park.

El metro está lejos, a quince minutos a pie.

The metro is far, fifteen minutes away on foot.

Me gusta caminar por aquí porque la gente es simpática.

I like walking around here because the people are friendly.

New words: panadería (bakery), quince minutos (fifteen minutes).
