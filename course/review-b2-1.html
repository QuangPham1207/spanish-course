READING 1 — FORMAL LETTER (UNA INSTANCIA AL AYUNTAMIENTO)

Context: A neighbourhood association writes a formal petition to the city council about the old trees on the riverbank. Formal register, subjunctives everywhere. Read it from top to bottom — the English below is only a check.

Con membrete de la asociación, la carta dice: «Registro 214: con esta instancia solicitamos que no se tale la arboleda de la ribera».

On association letterhead, the letter says: "File 214: with this petition we request that the riverbank trees not be felled."

«Si se talara toda la arboleda, el barrio sería un horno en verano».

"If all the trees were felled, the neighbourhood would be an oven in summer."

«Como si el calor no fuera bastante, también dicen que no hay dinero para el riego».

"As if the heat weren't enough, they also say there is no money for watering."

«Terminada la reunión del martes, les enviamos este texto con la firma de doscientas personas».

"Once Tuesday's meeting was over, we are sending you this text with the signature of two hundred people."

«Se adjunta el plano de la ribera: la tala de la arboleda vieja cambiaría todo el barrio».

"The map of the riverbank is attached: felling the old trees would change the whole neighbourhood."

«Les pedimos que lo lean con atención y les saludamos atentamente».

"We ask you to read it carefully and we send our sincere regards."

READING 2 — OPINION COLUMN (LA CARTA Y LA CIUDAD)

Context: An opinion column about the lost letter and what it says about the city. Read it quietly:

Si la carta hubiera llegado a tiempo, la señora habría conocido a su marido veinte años antes.

If the letter had arrived on time, the lady would have met her husband twenty years earlier.

Ojalá hubiera más carteros como aquel: él se tomaba su trabajo en serio.

If only there were more postmen like that one: he took his job seriously.

El detective, el cual ya tiene ochenta años, dice que lo que más recuerda es el sobre amarillo.

The detective, who is already eighty, says what he remembers most is the yellow envelope.

Se dice que la recompensa sigue esperando en un cajón de la comisaría.

They say the reward is still waiting in a drawer at the police station.

«Sin embargo —dice el autor—, lo importante no es la recompensa: es la historia».

"However —says the author—, what matters is not the reward: it's the story."

Reverdecer la ciudad y recordar sus historias es lo mismo: proteger lo que somos.

Re-greening the city and remembering its stories is the same thing: protecting what we are.

THE SENTENCES, BROKEN INTO PATTERNS

Re-read these mini-groups. Each one is a pattern you can reuse forever.

Imperfect subjunctive & sequences (L57)

  • Quiero que vengas mañana.I want you to come tomorrow.
  • Si tuviera tiempo, vendría.If I had time, I would come.

Unreal & mixed conditionals (L58)

  • Si hubiera llovido, habríamos salido.If it had rained, we would have gone out.
  • De haberlo sabido, habría venido.Had I known, I would have come.

Pluperfect subjunctive (L59)

  • Ojalá hubiera más carteros como aquel.If only there were more postmen like that one.
  • Me habló como si me conociera.He spoke to me as if he knew me.

The many values of se (L60)

  • Se venden casas.Houses for sale. (passive se)
  • Se le cayó el libro.The book fell from his hands. (accidental se)
  • Se dice que la recompensa sigue allí.They say the reward is still there. (impersonal se)

Non-finite clauses (L61)

  • Terminada la cena, salieron.Once dinner was over, they went out.
  • Al llegar a casa, llamó a su madre.On arriving home, he called his mother.

Nominalization & formal syntax (L62)

  • La tala de la arboleda cambiaría el barrio.Felling the trees would change the neighbourhood.
  • El riego de los parques cuesta dinero.Watering the parks costs money.

Word formation I: prefixes & suffixes (L63)

  • Reverdecer = re- (again) + verde + -cer.To re-green: make green again.
  • Imposible = im- (not) + posible.Impossible: not possible.

Word formation II: word families (L64)

  • Rápido → rápidamente; claro → claramente.Quick → quickly; clear → clearly.
  • Grande → grandísimo: very, very big.Big → huge (augmentative -ísimo).

Connectors & register (L65)

  • Llueve; sin embargo, salimos. (neutral: pero)It's raining; however, we're going out.
  • No había dinero; por lo tanto, no hubo riego. (neutral: por eso)There was no money; therefore, there was no watering.

Advanced pronouns (L66)

  • Se lo di ayer. (le + lo → se lo)I gave it to him/her yesterday.
  • El libro, el cual era viejo, era de mi madre.The book, which was old, belonged to my mother.
  • Lo que quiero es ver el rellano.What I want is to see the landing.
⚠️ One last reminder: the only thing a review asks is that you read. No writing, no exercises, no memorising. If a sentence felt easy today, that is progress — and watch the register: formal on paper (no obstante, se adjunta), neutral in speech (pero, también).
