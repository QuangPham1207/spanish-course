LESSON 59 — THE PLUPERFECT SUBJUNCTIVE IN ALL USES

Lesson goals:
  • Form it: hubiera / hubiese + participle (hubiera hecho, hubiese dicho).
  • Use it after como si and ojalá for unreal pasts.
  • Use it in past reporting: había pedido que hubiera hecho…
  • Read a memory piece: a family after the storm, acting as if nothing had happened.
  • Pick up words: huracán, escombros, cauce, arrasar, gotera, remendar, remordimiento.

Core vocabulary: hubiese, hubiera hecho, dicho, hecho, escrito, huracán, escombros, tejado, inundar, derrumbar, reconstruir, refugio, apagón, linterna, barro, charco, cauce, arrasar, azotea, gotera, trueno, relámpago, ráfaga, calma, remendar, fingir, disimular, suspiro, duelo, resignación, alivio, remordimiento, huella, cicatriz, evocar, revivir, atesorar
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): vela, ribera, tablón, sollozo

1. READING PASSAGE (COMO SI NADA HUBIERA PASADO)

Context: Three days after the hurricane, the narrator returns to the family house. The roof is gone, the street is rubble — but his mother serves dinner as if nothing had happened. Read each line; the English below it is only a check.

(Unreal pasts fill this memory — as if nothing had happened, I wish it hadn't… is today's grammar.)

Volví al pueblo tres días después del huracán, con una linterna en la mano y las botas llenas de barro hasta las rodillas.

I came back to the village three days after the hurricane, with a flashlight in my hand and my boots full of mud up to my knees.

La calle estaba llena de escombros: un tablón roto, tejas y un charco negro donde antes jugábamos por las tardes, sin miedo a nada.

The street was full of rubble: a broken plank, roof tiles and a black puddle where we used to play in the afternoons, afraid of nothing.

El tejado de casa había volado, y la primera noche dormimos en el refugio del colegio, entre sillas rotas y libros mojados.

The roof of the house had flown off, and the first night we slept in the school shelter, among broken chairs and wet books.

El río, salido de su cauce, parecía querer inundar la planta baja; en la ribera ya había logrado arrasar dos casas, y medio pueblo contenía la respiración.

The river, out of its course, seemed to want to flood the ground floor; on the bank it had already managed to sweep away two houses, and half the village held its breath.

De noche, una ráfaga logró derrumbar el muro del fondo, y por la azotea abierta se veía un cielo limpio, lleno de estrellas, como si pidiera calma.

At night, a gust managed to knock down the back wall, and through the open flat roof a clean sky could be seen, full of stars, as if asking for calm.

Mi madre, en cambio, barría el portal con fuerza, como si no hubiese pasado nada, como cada mañana de su vida.

My mother, in contrast, swept the doorway firmly, as if nothing had happened, like every morning of her life.

Había encendido dos velas porque el apagón duraba ya tres días y la comida se echaba a perder en la cocina.

She had lit two candles because the blackout had already lasted three days and the food was spoiling in the kitchen.

Cenamos en silencio, como si nadie hubiera dicho la palabra huracán.

We had dinner in silence, as if nobody had said the word hurricane.

Mi madre servía la sopa como si el viento no hubiera hecho nada.

My mother served the soup as if the wind hadn't done anything.

Mi padre prefería fingir que leía el periódico, mojado aún en sus manos temblorosas, sin pasar ni una página.

My father preferred to pretend he was reading the newspaper, still wet in his trembling hands, without turning a single page.

Yo intentaba disimular el miedo, pero cada trueno lejano y cada relámpago me hacían temblar bajo la mesa, con el corazón en la boca.

I tried to hide my fear, but every distant thunder and every lightning flash made me shake under the table, with my heart in my mouth.

Solo se oía la gotera de la cocina y, muy lejos, el sollozo de una vecina que había perdido todo, hasta las fotos de su boda.

All you could hear was the kitchen leak and, far away, the sobbing of a neighbour who had lost everything, even her wedding photos.

Después supe que mi abuela había pedido que mi tío hubiera hecho guardia en la puerta.

Later I learned that my grandmother had asked for my uncle to have stood guard at the door.

Ojalá el viento no hubiera hecho tanto daño, pensaba yo cada noche antes de dormir, con los ojos cerrados.

If only the wind hadn't done so much damage, I thought every night before sleeping, with my eyes closed.

Parecía una historia que alguien hubiera escrito con crueldad.

It seemed like a story someone had written with cruelty.

Mi madre no lloraba: decía que llorar era perder la calma, y se ponía a remendar cortinas rotas mientras cantaba en voz baja.

My mother didn't cry: she said crying was losing your calm, and she would start mending torn curtains while humming softly.

Pero en sus ojos había un duelo antiguo, una resignación sin alivio y un suspiro que no terminaba nunca, ni siquiera cuando dormía.

But in her eyes there was an old grief, a resignation with no relief, and a sigh that never ended, not even when she slept.

Con los años entendí su remordimiento: sentía no haber podido reconstruir la casa ella sola.

Over the years I understood her remorse: she felt she hadn't been able to rebuild the house alone.

Hoy suelo evocar aquella noche para revivir su valor: la casa guarda la huella del agua como una cicatriz, y yo prefiero atesorar su ejemplo y contar esta historia a mis hijos cuando pregunten por la huella de la pared.

Today I usually recall that night to relive her courage: the house keeps the mark of the water like a scar, and I prefer to treasure her example and tell this story to my children when they ask about the mark on the wall.

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. The form: hubiera / hubiese + participle

Put haber in the imperfect subjunctive (hubiera / hubiese — the L57 twins again) before a past participle. Regular participles end in -ado / -ido; the key irregulars are dicho, hecho, escrito.

hubiera / hubiese + participle → hubiera hecho, hubiese pasado, hubieran dicho
  • Como si no hubiese pasado nada.As if nothing had happened.
  • Como si nadie hubiera dicho la palabra huracán.As if nobody had said the word hurricane.
  • Como si el viento no hubiera hecho nada.As if the wind hadn't done anything.
  • Parecía una historia que alguien hubiera escrito con crueldad.It seemed like a story someone had written with cruelty.

2.2. Como si and ojalá about the past

Como si always opens an unreal scene: with a past scene it takes the pluperfect subjunctive (como si no hubiese pasado nada). Ojalá about the past works the same — it is a regret, something that can no longer change.

como si / ojalá + hubiera + participle → unreal or regretted past
  • Barría el portal como si no hubiese pasado nada.She swept the doorway as if nothing had happened.
  • Ojalá el viento no hubiera hecho tanto daño.If only the wind hadn't done so much damage.
  • Fingía leer, como si el periódico no hubiera dicho nada.He pretended to read, as if the newspaper had said nothing.
💡 Spain-vs-LatAm note: hubiera and hubiese are both correct everywhere — same twins as L57. In Latin America hubiera dominates in speech and hubiese can sound literary; in Spain you will hear both, with hubiera still first.

2.3. Past reporting: past main verb + que + pluperfect subjunctive

Extend the L57 sequence one step back. A main verb in a past perfect (había pedido) reporting something already finished before that takes the pluperfect subjunctive: había pedido que hubiera hecho…

past perfect main + que + pluperfect subjunctive → había pedido que hubiera hecho
  • Mi abuela había pedido que mi tío hubiera hecho guardia.My grandmother had asked for my uncle to have stood guard. (the guard duty was already over)
  • Dijeron que hubiera sido mejor salir antes.They said it would have been better to leave earlier.
  • Me había pedido que lo hubiera terminado antes del viernes.He had asked me to have finished it before Friday.
⚠️ Common mistakes: como si never takes the indicative: *como si no ha pasado nada is wrong — always como si no hubiera/hubiese pasado. And after a past main verb, don't use haya hecho (present perfect subjunctive): *había pedido que haya hecho is wrong — the past sequence needs hubiera hecho.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five unreal pasts. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. As if:

Como si nada hubiera pasado.

As if nothing had happened.

2. The regret:

Ojalá no hubiera hecho tanto daño.

If only it hadn't done so much damage.

3. The reported order:

Me había pedido que lo hubiera hecho antes del viernes.

He had asked me to have done it before Friday.

4. As if never:

Como si no hubiese llovido nunca.

As if it had never rained.

5. The cruel story:

Lo contó como si lo hubiera escrito un novelista cruel.

He told it as if a cruel novelist had written it.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: The mother and a neighbour talk in the doorway, three days after the storm.

Vecina: ¿Dormisteis en el refugio del colegio?

Neighbour: Did you sleep in the school shelter?

Madre: Sí, con la linterna y dos velas.

Mother: Yes, with the flashlight and two candles.

Vecina: Ojalá me hubieras avisado; habría ido contigo.

Neighbour: If only you'd told me; I'd have gone with you.

Madre: Fue todo tan rápido, como si no hubiera pasado el tiempo.

Mother: It was all so fast, as if time hadn't passed.

Vecina: Hay que remendar las cortinas con calma.

Neighbour: We must mend the curtains calmly.

Madre: Y reconstruir el muro antes de las lluvias.

Mother: And rebuild the wall before the rains.

Vecina: Entre todas lo haremos, ya verás.

Neighbour: Together we'll do it, you'll see.

No new words — the dialogue reuses refugio (shelter), linterna (flashlight), remendar (to mend), calma (calm) and reconstruir (to rebuild) from today's table.

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • The form: hubiera / hubiese + participle (hubiera hecho, hubiese pasado), with irregulars dicho, hecho, escrito.
  • Como si + ojalá: unreal or regretted pasts — como si no hubiese pasado nada, ojalá no hubiera hecho tanto daño.
  • Past reporting: past perfect + que + pluperfect subjunctive (había pedido que hubiera hecho guardia).

5.2. Further reading — la marca en la pared

Context: Years later, the mark of the water is still there. Read it quietly:

La huella del agua sigue en la pared del portal.

The mark of the water is still on the doorway wall.

Mi madre la llama su cicatriz favorita.

My mother calls it her favourite scar.

Dice que evocar el miedo le enseña a no tenerlo.

She says that recalling the fear teaches her not to feel it.

Yo prefiero atesorar sus palabras más que las fotos.

I prefer to treasure her words more than the photos.

Y cada vez que llueve, dormimos tranquilos: la casa ya demostró que aguanta.

And every time it rains, we sleep peacefully: the house has already shown it holds up.

No new words — it reuses huella (mark), cicatriz (scar), evocar (to recall) and atesorar (to treasure) from today's table.
