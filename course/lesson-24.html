LESSON 24 — COMMANDS FOR INSTRUCTIONS

Lesson goals:
  • Give instructions with the tú command: lava, corta, añade, mezcla.
  • Learn the eight irregular commands: di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé, ten, ven.
  • Recognise the polite usted / ustedes commands used in recipes and signs.
  • Read a recipe and follow directions in a city.

Core vocabulary: ingrediente, aguacate, cebolla, limón, sal, lavar, cortar, trozo, pelar, bol, añadir, mezclar, tenedor, necesario/necesaria, listo/lista, seguir, parar, doblar, finalmente, minuto, aceite, sartén, horno, fuego
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): hueso, pulpa, exprimir, batidora, cucharada, taza

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. The tú command (affirmative)

To tell a friend to do something, use the third-person present form — the same form as él/ella. Both -er and -ir verbs end in -e.

-ar → -a (hablar → habla)  ·  -er / -ir → -e (comer → come, vivir → vive)
  • Lava el tomate.Wash the tomato.
  • Corta la cebolla.Cut the onion.
  • Mezcla bien.Mix well.
  • Abre la ventana.Open the window.
  • Escribe tu nombre.Write your name.

2.2. The eight irregular commands

A few very common verbs do not follow the rule. Learn them as a chant: di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé, ten, ven.

VerbCommandVerbCommand

  • Haz la cena esta noche.Make dinner tonight.
  • Ven a mi casa a las ocho.Come to my house at eight.
  • Di la verdad.Tell the truth.
  • Sé amable con todos.Be kind to everyone.
  • Sal de casa temprano.Leave home early.

2.3. Attaching pronouns — and adding an accent

Object pronouns stick to the end of an affirmative command. When this makes the word longer, a written accent keeps the original stress.

  • Pela los aguacates. → Pélalos.Peel the avocados. → Peel them.
  • Corta la cebolla. → Córtala.Cut the onion. → Cut it.
  • Añade el limón. → Añádelo.Add the lemon. → Add it.
  • Quita el hueso. → Quítalo.Remove the stone. → Remove it.

2.4. Polite commands (usted / ustedes)

To be polite, or to speak to several people, the command changes. -ar verbs take -e; -er / -ir verbs take -a (the opposite vowel).

Verbusted (sing.)ustedes (pl.)
hablarhablehablen
comercomacoman
escribirescribaescriban
seguirsigasigan
  • Pase, por favor.Come in, please. (polite)
  • Siga recto y luego gire a la izquierda.Go straight on and then turn left. (polite)
  • Tomen asiento, señores.Take a seat, gentlemen. (to several people)
⚠️ Negative commands are different (advanced). "Don't do that" uses a form you will study at B1: No uses la batidora, No te pierdas. For now, just read and recognise them.
💡 Signs and notices often use the infinitive instead of a command: No fumar (no smoking), Empujar / Tirar (push / pull), No pisar el césped (keep off the grass).

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences of instructions. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Recipe instruction:

Corta las patatas y añádelas a la sartén.

Cut the potatoes and add them to the frying pan.

2. Direction:

Sigue recto y dobla a la derecha en la plaza.

Continue straight and turn right at the square.

3. Polite instruction:

Pase y tome asiento, por favor.

Come in and take a seat, please.

4. Irregular command:

Haz la comida y ven a las dos.

Make the food and come at two.

5. Reassuring instruction:

No te preocupes; es fácil. ¡Sigue así!

Don't worry; it's easy. Keep it up!

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: A mother explains a recipe to her son.

Mamá: Hoy te enseño a hacer tortilla de patatas.

Mum: Today I'll teach you how to make a potato omelette.

Hijo: ¿Qué hago primero?

Son: What do I do first?

Mamá: Pela las patatas y corta la cebolla. Luego pon todo en la sartén.

Mum: Peel the potatoes and cut the onion. Then put everything in the pan.

Hijo: ¿A fuego alto o lento?

Son: On a high or low heat?

Mamá: A fuego lento. Cuando esté blando, añade los huevos.

Mum: On a low heat. When it's soft, add the eggs.

Hijo: ¿Y después?

Son: And afterwards?

Mamá: Mezcla bien y cuélala por los dos lados. ¡Ten cuidado con el fuego!

Mum: Mix well and cook it on both sides. Be careful with the heat!

Hijo: Vale. ¡Es más fácil de lo que pensaba!

Son: Okay. It's easier than I thought!

New words in the dialogue: enseñar (to teach — Lesson 19), blando (soft), cuidado (care), lado (side), vale (okay, Spain).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • tú command: -ar → -a (habla); -er / -ir → -e (come, vive).
  • Irregulars: di, haz, ve, pon, sal, sé, ten, ven.
  • Pronouns attach and often add an accent: pélalos, córtala, añádelo.
  • Polite: -ar → -e, -er / -ir → -a (hable, coma, siga).
  • Negative commands and infinitive signs (No fumar) are for reading only.

5.2. Further reading — directions to the museum

Context: A short set of directions. Read it quietly:

Para ir al museo desde aquí, escucha con atención.

To go to the museum from here; listen carefully.

Sal del hotel y dobla a la izquierda.

Leave the hotel and turn left.

Sigue recto hasta el semáforo y cruza la avenida.

Continue straight to the traffic light and cross the avenue.

Pasa el parque y gira a la derecha en la iglesia.

Pass the park and turn right at the church.

El museo está enfrente; no te pierdas y disfruta la visita.

The museum is opposite; don't get lost and enjoy the visit.

New words: atención (attention), hasta (until, as far as — Lesson 08), disfrutar (to enjoy), visita (visit).
