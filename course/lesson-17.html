LESSON 17 — THE PAST SIMPLE, IRREGULAR

Lesson goals:
  • Learn the high-frequency irregular preterites: fui, tuve, hice, estuve, pude, puse, dije.
  • See the shared irregular endings (-e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron) with no accents.
  • Meet the special trio ser / ir / dar and ver, which behave differently.
  • Note the -ir stem-changing verbs that change only in the third person (pidió, durmió).

Core vocabulary: recorrido, sierra, regalo, móvil, sorpresa, gratis, inolvidable, creer, sacar (una foto), al día siguiente
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): sorprendido, amable, paisaje, conducir

1. READING PASSAGE (EL VIAJE A GRANADA)

Context: Nam tells the story of a trip to Granada, in southern Spain. The irregular past forms are in bold. Read each line; the English below is only a check.

(The Spanish is enough to follow the story.)

El año pasado fui a Granada con dos amigos.

Last year I went to Granada with two friends. (fui = I went, from ir)

Estuvimos allí cuatro días y lo pasamos muy bien.

We were there four days and had a great time. (estuvimos, from estar)

El primer día hicimos un recorrido por la ciudad.

On the first day we did a tour of the city. (hicimos, from hacer)

Vi la Alhambra y saqué muchas fotos.

I saw the Alhambra and took lots of photos. (vi, from ver)

Por la noche tuve hambre y buscamos un restaurante.

At night I was hungry and we looked for a restaurant. (tuve, from tener)

Pedimos tapas y el camarero nos trajo una sorpresa.

We ordered tapas and the waiter brought us a surprise. (trajo, from traer)

Nos dijo: «La comida es gratis hoy».

He told us: "The food is free today." (dijo, from decir)

No pude creerlo.

I couldn't believe it. (pude, from poder)

Al día siguiente fuimos a la sierra y vimos nieve.

The next day we went to the mountains and saw snow. (fuimos, vimos)

Quise comprar un regalo, pero no supe qué.

I wanted to buy a gift, but I didn't know what. (quise, from querer; supe, from saber)

Al final puse un mensaje en el móvil y vine a casa muy contento.

In the end I put a message on my phone and came home very happy. (puse, from poner; vine, from venir)

Fue un viaje inolvidable.

It was an unforgettable trip. (fue, from ser)

  • Me dio un regalo.He gave me a gift.

2.3. -ir stem-changing verbs: only the third person

Verbs like pedir, dormir, sentir change their stem in the third person only:

  • pedir → pedí, pediste, pidió, pedimos, pidieronto ask for (e→i in él/ellos)
  • dormir → dormí, dormiste, durmió, dormimos, durmieronto sleep (o→u in él/ellos)
  • Ella pidió la cuenta.She asked for the bill.
  • Ellos durmieron en un hotel.They slept in a hotel.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences that use irregular past forms naturally. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Say where you went:

El año pasado fui a España por primera vez.

Last year I went to Spain for the first time.

2. Talk about a stay:

Estuvimos tres días en un hotel pequeño.

We stayed three days in a small hotel.

3. Describe what you did:

Hicimos un recorrido y vimos la catedral.

We did a tour and saw the cathedral.

4. Express a surprise:

No pude creer la noticia.

I couldn't believe the news.

5. Close a memory:

Fue un viaje inolvidable; lo pasamos muy bien.

It was an unforgettable trip; we had a great time.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two colleagues talk about a holiday.

Marta: ¿Dónde estuviste de vacaciones?

Marta: Where were you on holiday?

Diego: Fui a Granada con mi familia. ¡Qué ciudad!

Diego: I went to Granada with my family. What a city!

Marta: ¿Y qué hicieron?

Marta: And what did you all do?

Diego: Vimos la Alhambra y comimos tapas todos los días.

Diego: We saw the Alhambra and ate tapas every day.

Marta: ¿Tuvieron buen tiempo?

Marta: Did you have good weather?

Diego: Sí, hizo sol. Un día fuimos a la sierra y vimos nieve.

Diego: Yes, it was sunny. One day we went to the mountains and saw snow.

Marta: ¡Qué bien! Yo no pude viajar este año.

Marta: How great! I couldn't travel this year.

Diego: El año que viene iremos juntos.

Diego: Next year we'll go together. (advanced: future tense.)

New words in the dialogue: la Alhambra (the famous palace in Granada), ¡Qué ciudad! (What a city!), el año que viene (next year).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • Many common verbs have an irregular stem + the endings -e, -iste, -o, -imos, -isteis, -ieron (no accents).
  • Key stems: tuv-, estuv-, hic-, pud-, pus-, sup-, quis-, vin-, dij-, traj-.
  • ser and ir share fui, fuiste, fue, fuimos, fueron; dar → di; ver → vi.
  • -ir stem-changers change only in the third person: pidió, durmió, sintió.
  • Watch the spelling: hizo, hicieron / dijeron, trajeron.

5.2. Further reading — a postcard from Oaxaca

Context: A short postcard that uses irregular preterites. Read it quietly:

¡Hola desde Oaxaca! El viaje fue largo, pero llegamos bien.

Hello from Oaxaca! The trip was long, but we arrived fine.

El primer día anduvimos por el centro y comimos mole.

On the first day we walked around the centre and ate mole.

Luego fuimos a las ruinas de Monte Albán: ¡qué paisaje!

Then we went to the ruins of Monte Albán: what a landscape!

Trajimos regalos para todos y sacamos muchas fotos.

We brought gifts for everyone and took lots of photos.

No pude dormir en el avión, pero valió la pena.

I couldn't sleep on the plane, but it was worth it.

New words: Oaxaca (a state and city in Mexico), mole (a famous Mexican sauce), ruinas (ruins), valer la pena (to be worth it).
