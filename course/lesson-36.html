LESSON 36 — SUBJUNCTIVE WITH EMOTIONS & IMPERSONAL EXPRESSIONS

Lesson goals:
  • Use the subjunctive after emotions: me alegra que…, me molesta que…, me preocupa que….
  • Use it after impersonal expressions: es importante que…, es necesario que…, es posible que….
  • See how a whole community reacts to a piece of news.
  • Pick up words: alegrarse, alegría, comunidad, autoridad, vecino, orgulloso.

Core vocabulary: alegrarse, alegría, emoción, sorprendente, normal, comunidad, autoridad, transporte, posible, probable, necesario, urgente, increíble, curioso/a, vecino/a, colaborar, tardar, bosque, contento/a, orgulloso/a, preocupar, raro/a, pena, vergüenza
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): centro de salud, acera, plaza

1. READING PASSAGE (LA REACCIÓN DE UN PUEBLO)

Context: A town reacts to the news that a new health centre will open. Read each line; the English below it is only a check.

(Every reaction — joy, worry, annoyance — pulls the verb into the subjunctive.)

Me alegra que el barrio tenga un parque nuevo.

I'm glad the neighbourhood has a new park.

Es triste que muchos jóvenes se vayan del pueblo.

It's sad that many young people leave the town.

Es importante que las autoridades escuchen a la gente.

It's important that the authorities listen to the people.

Me molesta que no haya más transporte público.

It annoys me that there isn't more public transport.

Es posible que el próximo año abran una escuela.

It's possible that they'll open a school next year.

Es mejor que empecemos ahora y no esperemos más.

It's better that we start now and don't wait any longer.

Es una pena que se pierdan tantos bosques cada año.

It's a shame that so many forests are lost every year.

Es sorprendente que la comunidad se organice tan rápido.

It's surprising that the community organises itself so quickly.

Me alegra que los vecinos colaboren sin esperar nada.

I'm glad the neighbours help without expecting anything.

Los vecinos arreglan la plaza y las aceras del pueblo.

The neighbours fix the town square and the pavements.

💡 Notice: the trigger is not a fact but a reaction. Es verdad que hay un parque (fact → indicative) vs Me alegra que haya un parque (emotion → subjunctive).

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. Emotions: verbs like gustar + que + subjunctive

These expressions put the person reacting in the indirect object (me, te, le…), followed by que + subjunctive.

me / te / le + alegra / molesta / preocupa / sorprende + que + subjuntivo
  • Me alegra que el barrio tenga un parque.I'm glad the neighbourhood has a park.
  • Me molesta que no haya transporte.It annoys me that there's no transport.
  • Me preocupa que se pierdan tantos bosques.It worries me that so many forests are lost.
  • Me da miedo que no llegue a tiempo.I'm afraid that he won't arrive on time.
  • Me da vergüenza que no participen.It embarrasses me that they don't take part.

2.2. Impersonal expressions: es + adjective + que + subjunctive

When an expression judges or values a situation, the following verb takes the subjunctive.

  • Es importante que las autoridades escuchen.It's important that the authorities listen.
  • Es necesario que empecemos ahora.It's necessary that we start now.
  • Es posible que abran una escuela.It's possible that they'll open a school.
  • Es probable que tarden un poco.They'll probably take a while.
  • Es urgente que arreglen los caminos.It's urgent that they fix the roads.
  • Es una pena que se pierdan tantos bosques.It's a shame that so many forests are lost.
💡 More impersonal triggers: es mejor que, es raro que, es increíble que, es curioso que, es normal que.

2.3. Fact vs reaction — a useful contrast

  • Es verdad que hay un parque.It's true that there's a park. (fact → indicative)
  • No es verdad que haya un parque.It's not true that there's a park. (denial → subjunctive)
  • Es normal que la gente quiera mejorar.It's normal that people want to improve. (value judgement → subjunctive)
⚠️ Common mistake: don't keep the indicative after an emotion. Say Me alegra que estés aquí, not Me alegra que estás aquí.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences of emotion and value. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Glad about something:

Me alegra que el barrio tenga un parque nuevo.

I'm glad the neighbourhood has a new park.

2. Something important:

Es importante que las autoridades escuchen a la gente.

It's important that the authorities listen to the people.

3. Annoyance:

Me molesta que no haya más transporte público.

It annoys me that there isn't more public transport.

4. A shame:

Es una pena que se pierdan tantos bosques.

It's a shame that so many forests are lost.

5. Surprise:

Es sorprendente que la comunidad se organice tan rápido.

It's surprising that the community organises itself so quickly.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two neighbours react to the news of a new health centre.

Elena: ¿Viste la noticia? Van a abrir un centro de salud.

Elena: Did you see the news? They're going to open a health centre.

Pablo: Me alegra mucho, porque antes no había nada.

Pablo: I'm really glad, because before there was nothing.

Elena: Es importante que llegue a todos los barrios.

Elena: It's important that it reaches all the neighbourhoods.

Pablo: Sí, y me molesta que hayan tardado tanto.

Pablo: Yes, and it annoys me that they've taken so long.

Elena: Bueno, más vale tarde que nunca.

Elena: Well, better late than never.

New words in the dialogue: más vale tarde que nunca (better late than never).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • Emotion + que + subjunctive: me alegra, me molesta, me preocupa, me sorprende, me da miedo.
  • Impersonal value + que + subjunctive: es importante, es necesario, es posible, es probable, es una pena.
  • Fact stays in the indicative: Es verdad que hay…; denial switches: No es verdad que haya….
  • The trigger is a reaction, not a fact.

5.2. Further reading — una comunidad que colabora

Context: A short note about neighbours working together. Read it quietly:

Es sorprendente que la comunidad se organice tan rápido.

It's surprising that the community organises itself so quickly.

Me alegra que los vecinos colaboren sin esperar nada.

I'm glad the neighbours help without expecting anything.

Es normal que la gente quiera mejorar su barrio.

It's normal that people want to improve their neighbourhood.

Es una pena que algunos no participen.

It's a shame that some don't take part.

New words: algunos (some), mejorar (to improve).
