LESSON 16 — THE PAST SIMPLE (PRETÉRITO INDEFINIDO), REGULAR

Lesson goals:
  • Start talking about the finished past — what happened yesterday, last week, last year.
  • Learn the regular preterite endings for -ar, -er and -ir verbs.
  • Recognise the time markers that usually signal the preterite: ayer, anoche, la semana pasada, el año pasado.
  • Notice that Spanish often drops the subject pronoun because the ending already says who did it.

Core vocabulary: pretérito indefinido, ayer, anoche, la semana pasada, el mes pasado, el año pasado, jefe/jefa, llamar, cenar, entrada, bocadillo, bar, feliz, olvidar, contar, recordar
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): durar, suceder, ocurrir, apenas, enseguida, terminar

1. READING PASSAGE (EL SÁBADO PASADO DE ELENA)

Context: Elena tells a friend what she did last Saturday. Every verb in bold is in the past simple. Read each line; the English below is only a check.

(The Spanish is enough to follow the story.)

El sábado pasado trabajé por la mañana en la oficina.

Last Saturday I worked in the morning at the office.

Me levanté a las siete y desayuné rápido en la cocina.

I got up at seven and had a quick breakfast in the kitchen.

A las ocho caminé hasta el centro y llegué antes de las nueve.

At eight I walked to the centre and arrived before nine.

Trabajé con mi jefa toda la mañana.

I worked with my boss all morning.

A las dos comí un bocadillo y bebí un café en un bar cerca del parque.

At two I ate a sandwich and drank a coffee in a bar near the park.

Por la tarde llamé a mi amiga Lucía y hablé con ella media hora.

In the afternoon I called my friend Lucía and spoke with her for half an hour.

Ella compró dos entradas para el cine.

She bought two tickets for the cinema.

Vimos una película nueva y nos gustó mucho.

We saw a new film and we liked it a lot. (vimos is irregular — see Lesson 17.)

Después cenamos en un restaurante pequeño: comimos pescado y bebimos agua.

Afterwards we had dinner in a small restaurant: we ate fish and drank water.

Volví a casa a las once, escuché música y leí un poco antes de dormir.

I came back home at eleven, listened to music and read a little before sleeping.

El domingo no trabajé: descansé, visité a mis padres y pasé el día con ellos.

On Sunday I didn't work: I rested, visited my parents and spent the day with them.

Fue un fin de semana tranquilo y feliz.

It was a calm and happy weekend. (fue is irregular — see Lesson 17.)

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. What the preterite does

The pretérito indefinido (past simple) describes finished actions at a specific moment in the past: I worked, she bought, we ate. If the action is complete, use the preterite.

  • Ayer trabajé ocho horas.Yesterday I worked eight hours.
  • Anoche cenamos en casa.Last night we had dinner at home.

2.2. Regular endings

💡 Accents: the yo and él/ella forms carry a written accent: hablé, habló / comí, comió. The other forms do not.

2.3. Time markers of the preterite

These words almost always point to a finished past action:

  • ayeryesterday
  • anochelast night
  • la semana pasada / el mes pasado / el año pasadolast week / last month / last year
  • hace dos díastwo days ago
  • La semana pasada visité a mi familia.Last week I visited my family.
  • Hace dos días llamé a Lucía.Two days ago I called Lucía.

2.4. Small spelling changes in the yo form

To keep the sound, some verbs change the spelling of the yo form only:

  • llegar → lleguéto arrive → I arrived
  • pagar → paguéto pay → I paid
  • buscar → busquéto look for → I looked for
⚠️ Advanced note (read only): leer and oír add an accent in some forms: leí, leíste, leyó, leyeron.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences for talking about the past. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Say what you did yesterday:

Ayer trabajé y después cené con mi familia.

Yesterday I worked and then had dinner with my family.

2. Talk about an outing:

El sábado pasado compré dos entradas para el cine.

Last Saturday I bought two tickets for the cinema.

3. Describe a trip:

El año pasado viajé a México y visité a mis abuelos.

Last year I travelled to Mexico and visited my grandparents.

4. Ask what someone did:

¿Qué hiciste anoche? — Estudié español.

What did you do last night? — I studied Spanish. (hiciste is irregular.)

5. Close a story:

Fue un día tranquilo y feliz.

It was a calm and happy day.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two friends talk on Monday about the weekend.

Diego: ¿Qué hiciste el sábado?

Diego: What did you do on Saturday?

Elena: Trabajé por la mañana y por la tarde llamé a Lucía.

Elena: I worked in the morning and in the afternoon I called Lucía.

Diego: ¿Y qué hicieron?

Diego: And what did you two do?

Elena: Compramos entradas y vimos una película nueva.

Elena: We bought tickets and saw a new film.

Diego: ¿Te gustó?

Diego: Did you like it?

Elena: Sí, me gustó mucho. Después cenamos pescado.

Elena: Yes, I liked it a lot. Afterwards we had fish for dinner.

Diego: ¡Qué buen fin de semana!

Diego: What a good weekend!

New words in the dialogue: ¿Qué hiciste? (What did you do? — irregular, Lesson 17), hicieron (you all did), buen (good, before a noun).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • The preterite describes finished past actions.
  • -ar: é, aste, ó, amos, asteis, aron. -er / -ir: í, iste, ió, imos, isteis, ieron.
  • Accents fall on yo and él/ella: hablé, habló, comí, comió.
  • Time markers: ayer, anoche, la semana pasada, el año pasado, hace dos días.
  • Some yo forms change spelling: llegué, pagué, busqué.

5.2. Further reading — a note from Hanoi

Context: Nam writes to a friend about his weekend. Read it quietly:

El viernes pasado no trabajé: fue un día libre.

Last Friday I didn't work: it was a day off.

Por la mañana caminé por el parque y desayuné en un bar pequeño.

In the morning I walked in the park and had breakfast in a small bar.

Después visité a mi abuela y comimos juntos.

Afterwards I visited my grandmother and we ate together.

Por la tarde estudié español y escuché música vietnamita.

In the afternoon I studied Spanish and listened to Vietnamese music.

Fue un día muy tranquilo y feliz.

It was a very calm and happy day.

New words: libre (free), juntos (together), vietnamita (Vietnamese).
