LESSON 18 — THE IMPERFECT & CONTRAST WITH THE PRETERITE

Lesson goals:
  • Learn the imperfect, the past tense for background, habits and descriptions.
  • Form it for -ar and -er / -ir verbs, and learn the only three irregulars: ser, ir, ver.
  • Understand the core contrast: imperfect = scene, preterite = event.
  • Read a childhood memory where the two tenses work together.

Core vocabulary: imperfecto, infancia, niñez, pueblo, vecino/vecina, preparar, perdido/perdida, época, mientras, antes, de niño/de niña, juguete, pelota
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): limonero, muñeca, soleado, añorar

1. READING PASSAGE (CUANDO ERA NIÑA)

Context: Elena remembers her childhood. The scene and the habits are in the imperfect; the single events are in the preterite. Read each line; the English below is only a check.

(Watch how the two past tenses paint one picture.)

Cuando era niña, vivía en un pueblo pequeño cerca del mar.

When I was a girl, I lived in a small village near the sea.

Mi casa era blanca y tenía un jardín con un limonero.

My house was white and had a garden with a lemon tree.

Todos los días mi hermano y yo jugábamos en la calle con los vecinos.

Every day my brother and I played in the street with the neighbours.

Por la mañana mi madre preparaba el desayuno y nosotros comíamos juntos.

In the morning my mother prepared breakfast and we ate together.

En verano hacía mucho calor y nadábamos en el mar.

In summer it was very hot and we swam in the sea.

A veces mi abuelo nos contaba historias por la noche.

Sometimes my grandfather told us stories at night.

Un verano pasó algo especial: encontramos un perro perdido.

One summer something special happened: we found a lost dog.

Lo llamamos Lucky y se quedó con nosotros diez años.

We called him Lucky and he stayed with us for ten years.

Yo era muy feliz en aquella época.

I was very happy in that time of my life.

Ahora vivo en la ciudad, pero siempre recuerdo mi infancia.

Now I live in the city, but I always remember my childhood.

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. What the imperfect does

The imperfecto does not say what happened; it paints how things were:

  • Describes scenes and people:La casa era blanca. Mi abuelo era amable.
  • Habits ("used to"):Todos los días jugábamos en la calle.
  • Age, time, weather in the background:Tenía ocho años. Era de noche. Hacía frío.
  • An action in progress:Mientras leía, mi madre cocinaba.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences that mix the two pasts naturally. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Set a childhood scene:

Cuando era niño, vivía en un pueblo pequeño.

When I was a child, I lived in a small village.

2. Describe a habit:

Todos los veranos nadábamos en el mar.

Every summer we used to swim in the sea.

3. Interrupt the scene with an event:

Mientras leía, alguien llamó a la puerta.

While I was reading, someone knocked at the door.

4. Describe age and time:

Tenía diez años y era de noche.

I was ten years old and it was night.

5. Contrast then and now:

Antes no había móviles; ahora todos tenemos uno.

Before there were no mobiles; now we all have one.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two friends remember how their town used to be.

Diego: ¿Cómo era tu barrio antes?

Diego: What was your neighbourhood like before?

Marta: Era tranquilo. Había una plaza y muchos árboles.

Marta: It was quiet. There was a square and lots of trees.

Diego: ¿Y qué hacían los niños?

Diego: And what did the children use to do?

Marta: Jugábamos en la calle hasta la noche.

Marta: We used to play in the street until night.

Diego: ¿Y qué pasó con la plaza?

Diego: And what happened to the square?

Marta: Un año construyeron un centro comercial allí.

Marta: One year they built a shopping centre there.

Diego: ¡Qué pena! Todo cambia.

Diego: What a shame! Everything changes.

New words in the dialogue: árbol (tree), construyeron (they built), centro comercial (shopping centre), cambiar (to change).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • Imperfect = background, habits, descriptions, age, weather, ongoing actions.
  • -ar: -aba, -abas, -aba, -ábamos, -abais, -aban. -er / -ir: -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían.
  • Only three irregulars: ser → era; ir → iba; ver → veía.
  • Preterite = a complete event: Ayer encontré un perro.
  • They combine: background in the imperfect, event in the preterite.

5.2. Further reading — the town I remember

Context: A short memory that mixes both past tenses. Read it quietly:

Cuando era pequeño, mi familia tenía una casa en la montaña.

When I was little, my family had a house in the mountains.

El aire era limpio y había un río cerca de la casa.

The air was clean and there was a river near the house.

Cada mañana caminábamos hasta el río y pescábamos.

Every morning we walked to the river and fished.

Un día vimos un oso y volvimos corriendo a casa.

One day we saw a bear and ran back home.

Nunca olvidaré aquella casa ni aquel verano.

I will never forget that house or that summer.

New words: aire (air), pescar (to fish), oso (bear), corriendo (running).
