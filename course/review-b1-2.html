REVIEW B1-2 — AN ADVANCED GRAMMAR-IN-CONTEXT TEXT

This is a re-reading lesson, not a test. There are no exercises and nothing to send. Read the two short texts and notice how much you already recognise.

What this review re-reads:
  • Lesson 39 — the conditional & si-clauses (types 1-2).
  • Lesson 40 — the future perfect (habré hecho) and conditional perfect (habría hecho).
  • Lesson 41 — the passive voice (ser + participle; passive vs impersonal se).
  • Lesson 42 — reported speech and tense backshift.
  • Lesson 43 — por vs para (full).
  • Lesson 44 — verb periphrases & aspect.

Almost every word here comes from Lessons 39-44. A few extra, very common words appear in context (listed after the texts). No need to memorise anything — re-reading is exactly how it settles in.

READING 1 — FEATURE (LA DECISIÓN DE LA DIRECTORA)

Context: A magazine feature on the theatre director from Lesson 44. A funding scandal threatens the premiere — and she must decide.

Si la subvención llega a tiempo, el estreno será una fiesta para toda la ciudad.

If the grant arrives on time, the opening will be a celebration for the whole city.

Si no llegara, la directora tendría que pedir un préstamo o posponer la gira.

If it didn't arrive, the director would have to take out a loan or postpone the tour.

El elenco lleva meses ensayando y sigue trasnochando en el camerino hasta la madrugada.

The cast has been rehearsing for months and keeps staying up late in the dressing room until the early hours.

Para ella, abandonar ahora sería una traición a su gente.

For her, giving up now would be a betrayal of her people.

Acaba de proponerse no volver a dudar: para el viernes ya habrá decidido.

She has just resolved not to doubt again: by Friday she will already have decided.

Caminó por el centro para pensar y se puso a retomar el guion con calma.

She walked through the centre to think and calmly set about taking up the script again.

Sin su valentía, la obra nunca habría llegado al telón final.

Without her courage, the play would never have reached the final curtain.

READING 2 — NEWS REPORT (EL ESTRENO Y EL ESCÁNDALO)

Context: The same week's news report: opening night, a leaked document, and what everyone said.

El teatro fue inaugurado en 1920 y fue derribado en parte por el terremoto.

The theatre was opened in 1920 and was partly knocked down by the earthquake.

Ayer se reanudó la función con un aforo completo: no quedaba ni una butaca.

Yesterday the performance resumed with a full house: not a single seat was left.

Un periodista preguntó si existía una filtración sobre la subvención.

A journalist asked whether there was a leak about the grant.

El portavoz dijo que todo estaba en orden y que al día siguiente presentarían las pruebas.

The spokesperson said everything was in order and that the next day they would present the evidence.

Según el titular de hoy, el tesorero había confesado un desvío de fondos ante el juez.

According to today's headline, the treasurer had confessed to a diversion of funds before the judge.

Se rumorea que dimitirá, pero la directora aseguró que el telón seguirá abriéndose cada noche.

Rumour has it he will resign, but the director assured everyone the curtain will keep opening every night.

El público, que llevaba meses esperando, ovacionó de pie: cada aplauso habrá merecido la pena.

The audience, who had been waiting for months, gave a standing ovation: every round of applause will have been worth it.

💡 Register note: a feature mixes inner decisions (conditionals, si-clauses, periphrases) with purpose and cause (por/para); a news report leans on the passive (fue inaugurado, se reanudó) and reported speech (dijo que…, preguntó si…). You are now reading both at once.

THE SENTENCES, BROKEN INTO PATTERNS

Re-read these mini-groups. Each one is a pattern you can reuse forever.

Conditional & si-clauses (L39)

  • Si la subvención llega a tiempo, el estreno será una fiesta.If the grant arrives on time, the opening will be a celebration.
  • Si no llegara, tendría que pedir un préstamo.If it didn't arrive, she would have to take out a loan.
  • Para ella, abandonar ahora sería una traición.For her, giving up now would be a betrayal.

Future perfect & conditional perfect (L40)

  • Para el viernes ya habrá decidido.By Friday she will already have decided.
  • Sin su valentía, la obra nunca habría llegado al telón final.Without her courage, the play would never have reached the final curtain.
  • Cada aplauso habrá merecido la pena.Every round of applause will have been worth it.

Passive voice (L41)

  • El teatro fue inaugurado en 1920.The theatre was opened in 1920.
  • Fue derribado en parte por el terremoto.It was partly knocked down by the earthquake.
  • Ayer se reanudó la función con un aforo completo.Yesterday the performance resumed with a full house.

Reported speech (L42)

  • Un periodista preguntó si existía una filtración.A journalist asked whether there was a leak.
  • Dijo que todo estaba en orden y que presentarían las pruebas.He said everything was in order and that they would present the evidence.
  • El tesorero había confesado un desvío ante el juez.The treasurer had confessed to a diversion before the judge.

Por vs para (L43)

  • Será una fiesta para toda la ciudad.It will be a celebration for the whole city.
  • Caminó por el centro para pensar.She walked through the centre to think.
  • Fue derribado en parte por el terremoto.It was partly knocked down by the earthquake.

Periphrases & aspect (L44)

  • El elenco lleva meses ensayando.The cast has been rehearsing for months.
  • Sigue trasnochando en el camerino.He/she keeps staying up late in the dressing room.
  • Acaba de proponerse no volver a dudar.She has just resolved not to doubt again.
⚠️ One last reminder: the only thing a review asks is that you read. No writing, no exercises, no memorising. If a sentence felt easy today, that is progress.
