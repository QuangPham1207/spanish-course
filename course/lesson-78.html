Lesson goals:
  • Follow a vacuna up the Amazon: the cadena de frío that saves lives.

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. Protocols wear se: se aísla, se rastrea

Health and science protocols erase people and keep actions: se vacuna (vaccination is done), se aisló (was isolated), se descubren (are discovered). Agent unknown or irrelevant.

  • Se vacuna en la clínica flotante.Vaccination happens at the floating clinic.
  • Se rastrearon los contactos y se aisló cada caso.Contacts were traced and each case isolated.
  • Allí se descubren planetas cada mes.Planets are discovered there every month.

2.2. Hypotheses: será, podría, llegaría

Science hedges with the future and conditional: será la capital (prediction), podría curar (possibility), llegaría (reported expectation). Certainty would be unscientific.

  • El desierto será la capital del cielo.The desert will be the capital of the sky.
  • La edición genética podría curar mañana.Gene editing may cure tomorrow.
  • La variante nueva podría llegar con las lluvias.The new variant may arrive with the rains.
⚠️ Common mistake: poder + infinitive never takes que. Say podría curar, not podría que cura. The infinitive follows directly.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: A health promoter, Rosa, visits a family in the hills with her dengue kit.

Rosa: Vengo por la campaña del dengue: se fumiga hoy y se revisa cada tacho.

Rosa: I'm here for the dengue campaign: fumigation today and every water tank checked.

Madre: ¿Y la vacuna de la niña? En la clínica dijeron que llegaría esta semana.

Mother: And my daughter's vaccine? At the clinic they said it would arrive this week.

Rosa: Llegó ayer en la cadena de frío. Traiga su historia clínica mañana.

Rosa: It arrived yesterday in the cold chain. Bring her medical record tomorrow.

Madre: Mi marido dice que por vídeo no se atiende bien.

Mother: My husband says video visits don't work well.

Rosa: La telemedicina no toca, pero sí escucha: el médico verá la variante de su tos.

Rosa: Telemedicine doesn't touch, but it does listen: the doctor will see your cough's variant.

Madre: Entonces confío: cobertura universal empieza en mi puerta.

Mother: Then I trust: universal coverage starts at my door.

New words in the dialogue: tacho (n.m., bucket/tank — Andean word for the water container) appears once for local colour. Everything else is from today's table.
