LESSON 20 — THE PRESENT PERFECT

Lesson goals:
  • Learn haber + past participle: he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han + -ado / -ido.
  • Talk about experiences and recent news with ya, todavía no, alguna vez, nunca, últimamente.
  • Learn the irregular participles: hecho, dicho, visto, puesto, escrito, roto, vuelto.
  • Understand how the present perfect contrasts with the preterite in Spain vs Latin America.

Core vocabulary: participio, experiencia, noticia, delicioso/deliciosa, ya, todavía, alguna vez, últimamente, portugués, romper, abrir, recientemente
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): aurora, jamás, recién, resolver

1. READING PASSAGE (LO QUE HE HECHO ESTE AÑO)

Context: Elena writes about her year so far. The present perfect is in bold — notice that the exact time is never given. Read each line; the English below is only a check.

(The present perfect links the past to now.)

Este año he hecho muchas cosas nuevas.

This year I have done many new things.

He viajado a tres países y he aprendido un poco de portugués.

I have travelled to three countries and I have learned a bit of Portuguese.

También he leído cinco libros en español.

I have also read five books in Spanish.

¿Has visto alguna vez una aurora? Yo todavía no la he visto.

Have you ever seen an aurora? I still haven't seen one.

Mi hermano ha vivido en Canadá dos años.

My brother has lived in Canada for two years.

¿Has probado la comida peruana? Sí, la he probado: es deliciosa.

Have you tried Peruvian food? Yes, I have tried it: it's delicious.

Últimamente no he dormido bien.

Lately I haven't been sleeping well.

¿Ya has terminado el trabajo? No, todavía no lo he terminado.

Have you finished the work yet? No, I still haven't finished it.

Hoy he hablado con mi jefa y me ha dado una buena noticia.

Today I have spoken with my boss and she has given me good news.

Nunca he estado en Europa, pero algún día iré.

I have never been to Europe, but some day I'll go. (advanced: future.)

💡 Why not the preterite? The present perfect never says when. It answers "What have you done?" not "What did you do yesterday?".

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. Form: haber + participle

he / has / ha / hemos / habéis / han + participio
Subjecthaber+ participle

túhashas comido

The participle is formed from the verb:

  • -ar → -adohablar → hablado; trabajar → trabajado
  • -er / -ir → -idocomer → comido; vivir → vivido
⚠️ The participle never changes with the subject: Ella ha trabajado, not ha trabajada.

2.3. Time markers of the present perfect

  • ya / todavía noalready / not yet
  • alguna vez / nuncaever / never
  • últimamente / recientementelately / recently
  • hoy / esta semana / este añotoday / this week / this year
  • Ya he comido. / Todavía no he comido.I've already eaten. / I haven't eaten yet.
  • ¿Has estado alguna vez en España? — No, nunca.Have you ever been to Spain? — No, never.

2.4. Present perfect vs preterite (and Spain vs Latin America)

Present perfectPreterite
He visitado Madrid.
I have visited Madrid (at some point).		Visité Madrid en 2019.
I visited Madrid in 2019.
Hoy he trabajado mucho.
Today I have worked a lot.		Ayer trabajé mucho.
Yesterday I worked a lot.
💡 Spain vs Latin America: in Spain, people often use the present perfect for things that happened today (Hoy he desayunado en un bar). In much of Latin America, the preterite is common even for today (Hoy desayuné en un bar). Both are correct — just be consistent.
⚠️ Common mistake: never mix a specific past time with the present perfect. Say Ayer trabajé, not Ayer he trabajado.

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five sentences for experiences and recent news. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Talk about experiences:

He viajado a muchos países, pero nunca he estado en Europa.

I have travelled to many countries, but I have never been to Europe.

2. Ask about experiences:

¿Has probado alguna vez la comida mexicana?

Have you ever tried Mexican food?

3. Talk about recent news:

Hoy he recibido una buena noticia.

Today I have received good news.

4. Say "not yet":

Todavía no he terminado el libro.

I still haven't finished the book.

5. Contrast with the preterite:

Esta semana he trabajado mucho; ayer trabajé hasta las diez.

This week I have worked a lot; yesterday I worked until ten.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two friends catch up on the year.

Diego: ¿Qué has hecho este año?

Diego: What have you done this year?

Marta: He viajado a México y he aprendido a cocinar.

Marta: I have travelled to Mexico and I have learned to cook.

Diego: ¿Has visto a Lucía últimamente?

Diego: Have you seen Lucía lately?

Marta: No, no la he visto. ¿Le ha pasado algo?

Marta: No, I haven't seen her. Has something happened to her?

Diego: Ha encontrado un trabajo nuevo en Madrid.

Diego: She has found a new job in Madrid.

Marta: ¡Qué buena noticia! Todavía no me lo ha dicho.

Marta: What good news! She still hasn't told me.

Diego: La llamó ayer, pero no contestó.

Diego: She called her yesterday, but she didn't answer. (specific past → preterite.)

New words in the dialogue: cocinar (to cook), contestar (to answer), ¿Le ha pasado algo? (Has something happened to her?).

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • haber + participle: he, has, ha, hemos, habéis, han + -ado / -ido.
  • Irregular participles: hecho, dicho, visto, puesto, escrito, roto, vuelto, abierto, resuelto, muerto.
  • No specific time is given: He visitado Madrid, but Visité Madrid en 2019.
  • Markers: ya, todavía no, alguna vez, nunca, jamás, últimamente, recientemente.
  • Spain uses it for today's events; much of Latin America prefers the preterite. Both are correct.

5.2. Further reading — an interview with a traveller

Context: A short interview that uses the present perfect naturally. Read it quietly:

— ¿Cuántos países has visitado?

— How many countries have you visited?

— He visitado más de treinta. He vivido en cuatro continentes.

— I have visited more than thirty. I have lived on four continents.

— ¿Y cuál ha sido tu mejor experiencia?

— And which has been your best experience?

— Una vez vi la aurora boreal en Islandia; nunca he olvidado ese momento.

— Once I saw the northern lights in Iceland; I have never forgotten that moment.

— ¿Has tenido miedo alguna vez? — Sí, pero nunca he dejado de viajar.

— Have you ever been afraid? — Yes, but I have never stopped travelling.

New words: continente (continent), Islandia (Iceland), miedo (fear), dejar de (to stop doing something).
