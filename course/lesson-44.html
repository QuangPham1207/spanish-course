LESSON 44 — VERB PERIPHRASES & ASPECT

Lesson goals:
  • Start, stop and repeat actions: acabar de, ponerse a, dejar de, volver a.
  • Show actions in progress and over time: seguir / llevar / ir + gerundio.
  • Show finished results: tener + participio (tengo ensayado…).
  • Read about a theatre troupe racing toward opening night.
  • Pick up words: ensayar, estreno, gira, telón, camerino, aplauso, ovación.

Core vocabulary: ensayar, ensayo, estrenar, estreno, gira, telón, madrugada, trasnochar, retomar, reanudar, abandonar, insistir, acostumbrarse, proponerse, posponer, camerino, aplauso, ovación, elenco, reparto, butaca, vestuario, maquillaje, acto, escena, guion, función, taquilla, entreacto, aforo, tramoya, apuntador
Extension words (read only, no need to memorize): bis, preestreno, reestreno

1. READING PASSAGE (EL ESTRENO)

Context: A small theatre company in Rosario has three weeks left before opening night. The director's diary is full of half-finished, restarted and finally completed actions.

(A periphrasis is two verbs working as one: the first verb adds the shade of meaning — just finished, starting up, still going — and the second carries the action.)

El elenco acaba de retomar los ensayos después de una semana de descanso.

The cast has just resumed rehearsals after a week off.

La directora se puso a reanudar el segundo acto a las ocho de la mañana.

The director got down to resuming Act Two at eight in the morning.

Llevan tres meses ensayando la misma escena del guion.

They have been rehearsing the same scene of the script for three months.

Los actores siguen trasnochando en el camerino hasta la madrugada.

The actors keep staying up late in the dressing room until the early hours.

La compañía tuvo que posponer la gira, pero no piensa abandonar el estreno.

The company had to postpone the tour, but it doesn't plan to abandon the opening.

El público ya va llenando las butacas: el aforo va creciendo cada día.

The audience is already filling the seats: the house is growing every day.

Vuelven a ensayar el final porque el apuntador insiste en acostumbrarse a la tramoya nueva.

They are rehearsing the ending again because the prompter insists on getting used to the new stage machinery.

La protagonista ya tiene ensayados todos sus vestuarios y el maquillaje de cada función.

The lead actress already has all her costume changes rehearsed and the makeup for each performance ready.

Cuando el telón se abra, dejarán de temblar y se propondrán disfrutar cada aplauso hasta la ovación.

When the curtain opens, they will stop shaking and will set out to enjoy every round of applause up to the ovation.

2. CORE VOCABULARY & GRAMMAR

2.1. Starting, stopping, repeating: acabar de · ponerse a · dejar de · volver a

Four periphrases with an infinitive cover the edges of an action: just done, suddenly starting, quitting, doing again. Note acabar de + present = "have just" (but + past = "ended up …-ing").

acabar de + inf. (just) · ponerse a + inf. (start suddenly) · dejar de + inf. (stop) · volver a + inf. (again)
  • Acaba de retomar los ensayos.She has just resumed rehearsals.
  • Se puso a reanudar el segundo acto.She set about resuming Act Two. (sudden, energetic start)
  • Dejarán de temblar cuando el telón se abra.They will stop shaking when the curtain opens.
  • Vuelven a ensayar el final.They're rehearsing the ending again.
  • Se proponen disfrutar cada aplauso.They intend to enjoy every round of applause. (proponerse + infinitive)

2.2. Actions over time: seguir · llevar · ir + gerundio

Three periphrases with a gerundio (-ando / -iendo) paint duration: seguir = still going, llevar = "have been …-ing for (time)", ir = gradual change.

seguir + ger. (keep) · llevar + time + ger. (have been) · ir + ger. (gradually)
  • Siguen trasnochando hasta la madrugada.They keep staying up late until the early hours.
  • Llevan tres meses ensayando la escena.They've been rehearsing the scene for three months.
  • El aforo va creciendo cada día.The house is growing every day. (gradual)
  • El público ya va llenando las butacas.The audience is already filling the seats.
💡 Spain-vs-LatAm note: these periphrases are identical everywhere. In speech, llevar + gerundio often replaces the English "have been …-ing" in all countries.

2.3. Finished results: tener + participio

Tener + participle (which agrees: ensayados, ensayadas) stresses a completed result you now hold — stronger than a plain perfect.

tener + participle (agrees) = finished and ready
  • Ya tiene ensayados todos sus vestuarios.She already has all her costume changes fully rehearsed.
  • Tenemos vendidas la mitad de las funciones.We already have half the performances sold out.
⚠️ Common mistakes: dejar de needs the de (dejaron de temblar, not dejaron temblar); llevar needs the time phrase (llevan tres meses ensayando); and tener + participle must agree (tiene ensayados).

3. GOLDEN PHRASES — READ FOR FAMILIARITY

Five periphrases. Read them once; no need to memorize.

1. Just finished:

Acaba de retomar los ensayos.

She has just resumed rehearsals.

2. Keep going:

Siguen trasnochando hasta la madrugada.

They keep staying up late until the early hours.

3. Duration:

Llevan tres meses ensayando la escena.

They've been rehearsing the scene for three months.

4. Doing again:

Vuelven a ensayar el final.

They're rehearsing the ending again.

5. Finished result:

Ya tiene ensayados todos sus vestuarios.

She already has all her costume changes fully rehearsed.

💡 Optional (skip if you like): whenever you feel like it, read all five out loud once. Not required.

4. SAMPLE DIALOGUE (READING)

Context: Two actors in the camerino before the preview.

Nico: Acabo de dejar de fumar. Estoy de los nervios.

Nico: I've just quit smoking. I'm a bundle of nerves.

Luz: Yo dejé de fumar hace años. Ponte a respirar conmigo.

Luz: I quit years ago. Start breathing with me.

Nico: Llevo una hora intentando calmarme y sigo temblando.

Nico: I've been trying to calm down for an hour and I'm still shaking.

Luz: El público ya va entrando. Tenemos vendidas todas las butacas.

Luz: The audience is already coming in. We have every seat sold.

Nico: Pues volvamos a repasar el guion una última vez.

Nico: Then let's go over the script one last time.

New words in the dialogue: repasar (to go over, to review) appears here in passing.

5. SUMMARY & FURTHER READING

5.1. Lesson recap

  • Edges: acabar de (just), ponerse a (sudden start), dejar de (stop), volver a (again) + infinitive.
  • Duration: seguir (keep), llevar + time (have been …-ing), ir (gradually) + gerundio.
  • Result: tener + participle (agrees): finished and ready.

5.2. Further reading — la gira que viene

Context: The troupe dreams past opening night. Read it quietly:

Si el estreno sale bien, retomarán la gira suspendida.

If opening night goes well, they will resume the suspended tour.

Ya se han propuesto no volver a posponer ninguna función.

They have already resolved never to postpone another performance.

En cada ciudad, el elenco volverá a ensayar el primer acto.

In each city, the cast will rehearse Act One again.

Llevarán meses viviendo entre camerinos, taquillas y entreactos.

They will have spent months living between dressing rooms, box offices and intervals.

Y cada noche dejarán de ser ellos para ser el reparto que el público ovaciona de pie.

And each night they will stop being themselves to become the cast the audience gives a standing ovation.

New words: de pie (standing) and suspender (to suspend) appear here in passing.
