# SPANISH COURSE SYLLABUS — A0 → B2

Goal: take the learner from **absolute beginner (A0)** to **CEFR B2** with a
**reading-first** method. All explanations are in English; the target language
is Spanish. Standard is **neutral Spanish**; where Spain and Latin America
differ, note both.

Method: build vocabulary, grammar and cultural knowledge **through reading**.
No homework, no tests, no submissions. Longer, real texts as the level rises.

Progression of content:
- **A1-A2**: everyday daily-life topics (greetings, food, family, city, work,
  travel, weather, health, feelings).
- **B1**: society, history and culture of the Spanish-speaking world.
- **B2**: philosophy, politics, art and longer argumentative/cultural texts.

Scale: ~60 lessons (2 per week ≈ 7-8 months). Each module ends with one
consolidated **review lesson by re-reading** (never a test).

Legend: `(adv.)` = above the current level, read for exposure only.

## LEVEL A1 — SURVIVAL & EVERYDAY BASICS
### MODULE A1-1: First contact
- **L01 — Greetings & the Spanish sounds.** *hola, buenos días, gracias, me
  llamo, ¿cómo estás?*; silent `h`, `ñ`, `ll`, `j`; stress & accent marks;
  `tú` vs `usted`.
- **L02 — Introducing yourself.** `ser`, `llamarse`, nationality and origin
  (`soy de…`), *encantado/a*.
- **L03 — Numbers, age & dates.** 0-100, `tener … años`, days, months.
- **L04 — Family & people.** Possessives (`mi, tu, su`), `tener`, family words.
- **L05 — Nouns: gender, articles & plurals.** `el/la`, `un/una`, `-s/-es`;
  exceptions.
- **Review A1-1** — re-read a first-contact dialogue.

### MODULE A1-2: Everyday life
- **L06 — The present tense.** Regular `-ar / -er / -ir` verbs, subject
  pronouns.
- **L07 — Food & ordering.** `querer, comer, beber`; café, market, restaurant;
  tapas vs antojitos (cultural note).
- **L08 — Home & daily routine.** Reflexive verbs (`levantarse, ducharse`);
  telling time.
- **L09 — Shopping & prices.** Numbers in use, `¿cuánto cuesta?`, colors,
  clothes.
- **Review A1-2** — re-read a market scene.

### MODULE A1-3: Getting around
- **L10 — The city & directions.** `estar`, `hay`, `ir a`, prepositions of
  place.
- **L11 — Transport & travel basics.** `ir/tomar`, tickets, timetables.
- **L12 — Weather & seasons.** `hace frío/calor`, `llueve`, months and
  climates of Spain vs Latin America.
- **L13 — Likes & hobbies.** `gustar` / `encantar`, sports, music, free time.
- **L14 — A1 consolidation.** A longer beginner reading tying A1 together.
- **Review A1** — re-read an A1 dialogue + short text.

## DESIGN NOTES (for the tutor / author)
- Every lesson follows the 5-part structure in `AGENTS.md` §5 and is a single
  HTML file modeled on `course/lesson-01.html`.
- Vocabulary and grammar must respect the level: do not use subjunctive
  before L29, past tenses before L15, etc. Label out-of-level material
  `(advanced)`.
- Two-tier format everywhere: Spanish line + English meaning. At B2, known
  words may be left unglossed to encourage extensive reading, but the AGENTS
  contract keeps the two-tier block by default.
- At B1+, replace generic topics with real stories, specific people and
  present-day debates. Prefer the less-obvious angle.
- Review lessons are **re-reading** consolidations: no questions, no grading.
- Numbers (L01…L55) are a guide, not a cage. Add or split lessons when a topic
  needs it, and keep `course/data/lesson-XX.json` in sync.
